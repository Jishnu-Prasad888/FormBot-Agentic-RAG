## Passbook & Daily Transaction Codes [4] 
These abbreviations are used directly in physical SBI passbooks, online receipts, and automated SMS alerts: [1, 5, 6] 

* a/c: Account
* adj: Adjustment
* amt: Amount
* Ar: Arrear
* bal: Balance
* Capn: Capitalization
* chg / ch: Charge
* chq: Cheque
* clos: Closure
* coll: Collection
* comm: Commission
* COR / CORR: Correction
* CR: Credit
* csh: Cash
* dep: Deposit
* dft: Draft
* dish / dsh: Dishonour
* DR: Debit
* DoB: Date of Birth
* eft: Electronic Fund Transfer
* Inop: Inoperative
* ins: Insurance
* int / in: Interest
* lon / ln: Loan
* min: Minimum
* MOD Bal: Total balance including linked Multi Option Deposit account
* OS: Outstanding
* P&T: Postage & Telegram
* Pos: Point of Sale
* Pr: Principal
* proc: Processing Charge
* rd: Recurring Deposit
* ret / rtn: Return
* Rnd: Round off
* sb: Savings Bank
* SC: Short Credit
* SI / So / SORD: Standing Instruction
* S/D/W/H/o: Son / Daughter / Wife / Husband of
* tr / trf / xfer / TRT: Transfer
* TT: Telegraphic Transfer
* txn: Transaction
* Wdl: Withdrawal [5, 7, 8, 9, 10] 

## YONO & Digital Banking Interface Terminology [11] 
These specific acronyms govern SBI's digital platforms and Corporate Internet Banking (CINB) architectures: [2] 

* CINB: Corporate Internet Banking
* CMP: Cash Management Product
* INB: Internet Banking
* IVR: Interactive Voice Response
* MBS: Mobile Banking Services
* SCF: Supply Chain Finance
* YBBI: Yono Business Branch Interface
* YONOB: Yono Business [1, 2] 

## Core Banking & Compliance Identifiers
These codes are mandatory structural components of every SBI bank account profile and regulatory documentation layout: [1, 12] 

* AEPS: Aadhaar Enabled Payments Switch
* APBS: Aadhaar Payment Bridge System
* ASBA: Application Supported by Blocked Account
* BSBD: Basic Savings Bank Deposit
* CBS: Core Banking Solution
* CIF: Customer Information File
* EMI: Equated Monthly换 Installment
* IFSC: Indian Financial System Code
* IMPS: Immediate Payment Service
* KYC: Know Your Customer
* MICR: Magnetic Ink Character Recognition
* NACH: National Automated Clearing House
* NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer
* NPA: Non-Performing Asset
* OD: Overdraft
* PPF: Public Provident Fund
* RTGS: Real Time Gross Settlement
* SARFAESI: Securitization and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act
* UPI: Unified Payments Interface [1, 5, 7, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17] 

## Institutional & Treasury Frameworks [12] 
These terms are utilized internally by SBI's corporate management, board committees, and treasury offices: [13] 

* AGM: Annual General Meeting
* ALCO: Asset Liability Committee
* ALM: Asset Liability Management
* AMRMS: Audit and Risk Monitoring Mechanism
* ARC: Asset Reconstruction Company
* BCSBI: Banking Codes and Standards Board of India
* BPLR: Benchmark Prime Lending Rate
* BSR: Basic Statistical Returns
* IOI: Inter Office Account
* IRC: Inward Remittance Cell
* MSME: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
* PSB: Public Sector Bank
* SLR: Statutory Liquidity Ratio [1, 12, 13, 18, 19, 20]
