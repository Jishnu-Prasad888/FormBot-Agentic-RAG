# Loan Against Time Deposit

Get access to funds whenever you need them—whether for a family vacation, medical emergency, or any other financial requirement—without breaking your Time Deposit.

---

## Features

* Loan up to **90% of the value of your Time Deposit**
* Available as:

  * **Demand Loan**
  * **Overdraft Facility**
  * *(Only Demand Loan facility is available against non-callable deposits)*
* Low interest rates with **daily reducing balance interest calculation**
* **Zero processing charges**
* **No prepayment penalties**
* **Online Overdraft Limits (Against Fixed Deposits)**

  * Minimum: **₹5,000**
  * Maximum: **₹5 Crore**

### Margin Requirements

#### Demand Loans & Overdrafts

* Minimum margin of **10%** for:

  * Loan tenure up to 5 years, or
  * Remaining maturity period of the deposit,
  * Whichever is earlier.

#### Demand Loans (Tenure > 5 Years)

* For loan tenure exceeding 5 years and up to 10 years (or remaining deposit maturity period, whichever is earlier):

  * Minimum margin: **15%**

### Security

* Lien on the underlying Time Deposit

### Loan Types

* Demand Loan
* Overdraft

### Available Channels

#### YONO & Internet Banking (INB)

* Only **Overdraft Facility** available
* Applicable only against **TDR/STDR** issued through YONO/INB channels

#### Branches

* Both **Demand Loan** and **Overdraft Facility** available

### Interest Rate

* **1% above the applicable Time Deposit rate**

---

## Eligibility

### Through Branches

Customers holding the following SBI deposits can avail the loan:

* TDR/STDR
* Recurring Deposits (RD)
* NRE Deposits
* NRO Deposits
* FCNR(B) Deposits

Available for:

* Single account holders
* Joint account holders

### Through YONO & Internet Banking

Only **Overdraft Facility** is available for:

* Single account holders
* Against TDR/STDR deposits
* Deposits issued through YONO/INB channels

**Joint account holders are currently not eligible online.**

---

## Repayment

### Demand Loan

* Tenure: Remaining maturity period of the deposit or **120 months**, whichever is earlier.
* If not repaid before deposit maturity, the loan account will be liquidated using the Time Deposit proceeds.

### Overdraft

* Maximum tenure: **5 years** or remaining maturity period of the deposit, whichever is earlier.
* Renewal available through branch visit after expiry.
* Only branch-opened overdraft accounts can be renewed.
* Overdrafts opened through YONO/INB must be liquidated at deposit maturity if not repaid earlier.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## 1. Who is eligible for SBI Loan Against Fixed Deposit?

### Online

* Individual customers holding:

  * TDR
  * STDR
  * e-TDR
  * e-STDR
* Must be in a **single name only**.

### Branch

* Joint account holders are eligible.
* Loans can also be sanctioned against:

  * RD/e-RD
  * NRE Deposits
  * NRO Deposits
  * RFC Deposits
  * FCNR(B) Deposits

---

## 2. What security is required?

### Online

* e-TDR/e-STDR/TDR/STDR deposits

### Branch

* TDR/STDR
* RD/e-RD
* NRE/NRO Deposits
* RFC Deposits
* FCNR(B) Deposits

> Online loans against RD, NRE/NRO, RFC, and FCNR(B) deposits are currently not available.

---

## 3. Is Demand Loan available online?

No.

* Online facility is restricted to **Overdraft only**.
* For a Demand Loan, customers must visit a branch.

---

## 4. How much overdraft can be availed?

### Online

* **90%** of e-STDR/STDR value
* **75%** of e-TDR/TDR value

### Branch

* Up to **90%** of the value of the underlying security

---

## 5. What is the interest rate?

* **1% above the applicable Time Deposit rate**

---

## 6. What is the maximum overdraft limit?

### Online

* Up to **₹5 Crore**

### Branch

* No specific upper limit prescribed

---

## 7. Is there a minimum loan amount for online overdraft?

### Online

* Minimum loan amount: **₹25,000**

### Branch

* No specific minimum loan amount

> **Note:** Some product information mentions ₹5,000 as the minimum online overdraft amount. Customers should verify the current applicable limit with SBI.

---

## 8. Can an overdraft be availed jointly online?

No.

* Online facility is available only for single-name deposit holders.
* Joint account holders must visit a branch.

---

## 9. What is the repayment period?

### Online Overdraft

#### Against e-STDR/STDR

* Up to **5 years** or residual deposit period, whichever is lower
* Subject to a minimum of **6 months**

#### Against e-TDR/TDR

* Up to **3 years** or residual deposit period, whichever is lower
* Subject to a minimum of **6 months**

### Branch-Sanctioned Loans

* Repayment schedule is determined based on the borrower's repayment capacity.

---

## 10. How can the facility be availed?

### Online

1. Log in to SBI Internet Banking.
2. Navigate to **e-Fixed Deposit** section.
3. Select **Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit**.

### Branch

* Visit any SBI branch and apply.

---

## 11. Can online overdraft be availed against Tax Saving Deposits?

No.

Online overdraft is **not available** against:

* MODS (Multi Option Deposit Scheme)
* Tax Saving Deposits
* NRE TDR/STDR
* Recurring Deposits (RD)
