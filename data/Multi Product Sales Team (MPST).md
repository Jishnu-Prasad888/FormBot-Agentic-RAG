The Multi Product Sales Team (MPST) in the State Bank of India (SBI) acts as a high-velocity marketing and customer acquisition engine. [1] 
The comprehensive breakdown of everything related to MPST covers its structure, functions, and place within the bank.
## 🏢 Structural Alignment & Co-location

* The Shared Desk Policy: MPST branches are almost always physically housed within the premises of RASMECCC (Retail Assets and Small and Medium Enterprises City Credit Centers).
* The Strategy: While RASMECCC handles the back-end processing, evaluation, and sanctioning of credits, the MPST sits right next to it as the front-end customer interaction and onboarding force.
* Administrative Hierarchy: MPST cells answer directly to their localized Regional Business Offices (RBO) and Administrative Offices (AO) rather than individual neighborhood branches. [1] 

## 💼 Core Functions & Responsibilities

* The "Multi-Product" Mandate: They do not focus on just one line of business. An MPST officer handles retail liabilities (savings/current accounts), retail assets (home loans, car loans), and SME offerings.
* Targeted Outreach: Unlike a standard walk-in branch teller, MPST personnel are actively expected to engage in outdoor sales, corporate salary account tie-ups, and builder-site visits.
* Third-Party Product (TPP) Cross-Selling: MPST is one of SBI's primary drivers for non-interest income. They push partner products like SBI Mutual Funds (partnered with Amundi), SBI Life Insurance, and SBI Cards. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

## ⚙️ How It Impacts Customers & Applicants

* Faster TAT (Turnaround Time): Because MPST operates right beside the credit processing team (RASMECCC), submitting a home or business loan application through an MPST agent usually bypasses standard branch delays.
* Single Point of Contact: An MPST representative often acts as a dedicated relationship manager who will pick up document photocopies directly from your home or office.
* Zero Cash Operations: MPST offices do not handle everyday retail banking operations like cash deposits, withdrawals, passbook printing, or draft issuances. [1] 

## 👥 Internal Career & Employee Perspective

* The Sales Pressure: Employees assigned to MPST carry strict, high-volume target metrics across multiple asset and liability divisions simultaneously.
* The Daily Workflow: It involves a balance of digital document verification, tracking leads generated via the YONO SBI app, and field inspections. [3]

Through the Multi Product Sales Team (MPST), SBI functions as a direct, door-to-step sales and onboarding system for home and auto loans.
------------------------------
## 1. Loan Products Handled by MPST

* SBI Home Loans: Covers standard home purchases, plots, SBI MaxGain (home loan with overdraft), and home renovation loans.
* SBI Auto Loans: Covers new car loans, certified pre-owned car financing, and superbike loans.

------------------------------
## 2. The Step-by-Step Processing Journey

[MPST Representative] ──> [Pre-Sanction Inspection] ──> [RASMECCC Processing Hub] ──> [Disbursal]
  (Onboards Customer)          (Verifies Property/Car)        (Scores & Approves Credit)      (Funds Transferred)


   1. Lead Capture: MPST gets your details via the SBI YONO App or direct field sourcing.
   2. Doorstep Onboarding: An MPST agent visits your home or office to collect documents and signatures.
   3. Pre-Sanction Inspection (PSI): The MPST agent physically verifies your residential address and visits the property/car dealership.
   4. RASMECCC Handover: MPST uploads your file directly to the co-located RASMECCC credit hub for legal scrutiny and valuation.
   5. Sanction & Disbursal: Once RASMECCC approves the loan, your home branch or the centralized loan hub releases the funds.

------------------------------
## 3. Key Advantages for the Borrower

* Speed: Co-location with RASMECCC credit officers reduces communication delays and shortens turnaround times.
* Doorstep Convenience: You do not need to stand in long queues at a traditional, crowded SBI branch.
* Negotiation Directness: MPST personnel have a direct line to credit sanctioning authorities to pitch unique or borderline cases.

------------------------------
## 4. Required Document Checklist
To keep your application moving quickly through the MPST, prepare these standard documents:

| Document Category | Home Loan Requirements | Auto Loan Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Income Proof (Salaried) | 3 months payslips, 2 years Form 16, 6 months bank statements | 3 months payslips, 1 year Form 16, 6 months bank statements |
| Income Proof (Self-Employed) | 3 years ITR, Balance Sheet, P&L statement, Business license | 2 years ITR, Balance Sheet, P&L statement, Shop establishment certificate |
| Asset/Collateral Proof | Registered Sale Agreement, Land Tax receipts, Builder No-Objection Certificate (NOC) | Proforma Invoice from the car dealer, RTO allocation details |
| KYC | PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport size photos | PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport size photos |
