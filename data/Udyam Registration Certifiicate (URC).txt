What is Udyam Registration Certificate?

Udyam registration is a mandatory certification from the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, marking an enterprise’s commitment to growth and adherence to regulatory standards.

The Twelve-Digit Code:

At the heart of Udyam registration lies the issuance of a unique twelve-digit Udyam registration number (URN). This numerical identifier becomes the enterprise’s digital signature, distinguishing it in the vast landscape of businesses. This URN, akin to a digital passport, serves as a gateway for MSMEs to access a myriad of benefits aimed at fostering their development.

Significance and Launch:

Since its launch in July 2020, Udyam registration has become a beacon for MSMEs navigating the complexities of the business world. The significance lies not only in the certification itself but also in the subsequent advantages that propel enterprises towards sustainable growth. It marks a commitment to transparency, compliance, and a willingness to contribute to the economic tapestry.

Eligibility Criteria

The classification is structured around two key parameters, investment and turnover, serving as the foundation for enterprises to gauge their eligibility.

Investment and Turnover Categories:

The eligibility criteria for Udyam registration are delineated based on the size and scale of enterprises. Here’s a snapshot:

Type of Enterprise	Investment Limit	Turnover Limit
Micro Enterprises	Up to Rs. 1 crore	Up to Rs. 5 crore
Small Enterprises	Up to Rs. 10 crore	Up to Rs. 50 crore
Medium Enterprises	Up to Rs. 50 crore	Up to Rs. 250 crore
Enterprises must align their financials with these categories, ensuring accurate self-assessment before proceeding with Udyam registration. Furthermore, the enterprise must function as a legal entity, operating within the manufacturing or service sectors.

Inclusive Approach:

Udyam registration extends its arms wider, embracing not only traditional manufacturers and service providers but also wholesalers and retailers. This inclusivity broadens the scope of MSMEs that can benefit from government initiatives, fostering a more robust and diverse economic landscape.

Documents Required for Udyam Registration

One of the standout features of Udyam registration is the minimal documentation required. The process operates on a self-declaration basis, streamlining the bureaucratic hurdles that often accompany such certifications. The primary document needed is the Aadhaar number of the enterprise’s owner or authorized signatory.

1. Additional Mandatory Details: While the Aadhaar number takes centre stage, a few other essential details must accompany the registration application:

2. PAN and GSTIN: To complete the Udyam Aadhaar registration, enterprises need to provide their PAN (Permanent Account Number) and GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number).

3. Business Address Proof: Simple yet crucial, the business address proof can be in the form of property tax receipts or utility bills (telephone, electricity, etc.).

4. Bank Account Details: Transparency in financial transactions is paramount. Enterprises need to furnish their bank account details, including the account number and IFSC code.

Government Schemes Especially for Udyam Registered Firms

Udyam registration opens up access to several government schemes designed to support and promote MSMEs in India. These initiatives provide financial assistance, skill development, technological upgrades, and market access to enhance the growth and sustainability of Udyam registered businesses. Here are few schemes outlined:

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE): This initiative, under the Government of India and SIDBI, offers collateral-free loans up to ₹5 Crore with an 85% loan guarantee. It has supported over 67 lakh MSMEs, aiding in technology upgradation, market development, and financial inclusion. In 2023, ₹1.46 Lakh Crore worth of guarantees were approved, benefiting MSMEs across multiple sectors.
Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP): PMEGP provides loans and working capital (₹25 Lakh for manufacturing and ₹10 Lakh for service sector MSMEs) to foster entrepreneurship and employment. Since its inception in 2008, the scheme has supported over 9.29 lakh micro-enterprises with a Margin Money subsidy of ₹34,517 Crore, generating employment for around 78.36 lakh individuals.
Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP): Launched in 2022 with World Bank assistance, RAMP aims to improve MSME access to global markets and credit. The scheme also focuses on enhancing the effectiveness of existing government initiatives and promoting sustainable and women-led MSMEs. With an allocated budget of ₹6000 Crore for five years, it is expected to benefit over 5.5 lakh MSMEs, strengthening their growth potential and international competitiveness.

What is the Udyam Registration Process? A Step-By-Step Guide
STEP 1: Visit the Official Udyam Registration Website
Go to the official Udyam Registration Portal to begin your registration process.

STEP 2: Select Your Entrepreneur Status
If you are a new entrepreneur yet to register as an MSME or if you already hold an EM-II, choose the appropriate option from the homepage.

STEP 3: Enter Aadhaar Details for Verification
Provide your Aadhaar number and the business name as it appears on the Aadhaar card. Click the “Validate & Generate OTP” button to proceed with the Aadhaar verification.

STEP 4: Verify Your PAN Number to Check Validity
Next, enter your PAN number and click the “Validate” button to verify your PAN.

STEP 5: Fill in Additional Information Required
After verifying your Aadhaar and PAN, complete the additional information section. This includes selecting your organization type, specifying business activities, providing your address and bank details, and entering other relevant information. Ensure all details are accurate and up-to-date.

STEP 6: Review and Submit the Form After Completion
Once you have filled out the form with all necessary information, review it carefully before submission. After submitting, you will receive a unique registration number and a certificate. The certificate will include a QR code, allowing access to your enterprise’s details.
