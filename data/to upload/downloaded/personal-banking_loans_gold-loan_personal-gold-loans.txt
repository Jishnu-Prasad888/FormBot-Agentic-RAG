1. What are the purposes for which SBI Personal Gold Loan can be availed?
a) SBI Personal Gold Loan is a general-purpose Loan scheme available to individuals, against security of gold ornaments & specially minted gold coins (max 50 grams) sold by Banks.
b) It can be taken for meeting expenses of sudden medical exigency, margin money for Home Loan and/or Education Loan, travel plans including consumption purpose.
2. How much time Bank takes to sanction Personal Gold Loan?
Personal Gold Loan is normally sanctioned on the same day of application and loan amount is credited to customer account.
3. I am not an existing customer of SBI, can I apply for Personal Gold Loan?
Yes, you can apply for Personal Gold Loan. Only thing is that you need is to open a Saving Account with KYC documents with SBI where loan amount would be deposited
4. Who is eligible for SBI Personal Gold Loan?
nyone of 18 years and above age with steady source of income.
5. Is Submission of proof of income by Borrower necessary?
No, it is not necessary, but she/he should have the capacity to repay the loan.
6. What are the documents required for availing SBI Personal Gold Loan?
Two Passport Size Photographs.
Proof of identity with proof of Address (OVD)
7. What is the minimum & maximum loan amount that can be availed?
Minimum Loan amount is Rs. 20000/- and maximum is Rs. 50.00 lacs per individual.
8. What are the processing charges?
The product wise processing charges are as under:
For 3 Months Bullet Repayment – ₹ 200 + applicable GST.
For 6 Months Bullet Repayment – ₹ 300 + applicable GST.
For 12 Months Bullet Repayment – 0.50% of loan amount, subject to minimum ₹ 500 and maximum ₹ 10,000 + applicable GST.
SBI Personal Gold Loan (EMI) - 0.50% of loan amount subject to minimum ₹ 500 + applicable GST.
SBI Liquid Gold Loan (overdraft): 0.50% of loan amount, subject to maximum ₹ 10,000 + applicable GST.
9. What is the repayment period of the loan?
For SBI 3 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan – 3 Months
For SBI 6 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan – 6 Months
For SBI 12 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan – 12 Months
For SBI Personal Gold Loan - EMI Based – 36 Months
For Gold Loan Overdraft – 36 Months
10. How can I repay my Personal Gold Loan?
Personal Gold Loan is available as Demand Loan and Bullet Repayment facility. You can repay Personal Gold Loan through regular EMI and Interest every month.
Demand loan is payable through monthly EMI.
In Bullet Repayment Gold Loan, customers have the option of paying principal and interest on or before maturity of loan.

12. How much loan amount is available per gram of Gold?
The amount of loan is decided on the basis of Gold purity i. e. on the basis of carat (22, 20, 18) and net weight. Loans are not granted against the security of Primary Gold i. e., 24 carats gold bars and biscuits.
13. How much margin is required to take SBI Personal Gold Loan?
Variant wise applicable margins are as under:
Gold Loan (EMI based): 28%
3 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan : 30%
6 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan: 30%
12 Months Bullet Repayment Gold Loan: 35%
Gold Loan Overdraft – 26%
These margins are applicable irrespective of Loan amount.
14. Can I avail loan against Gold Coins sold by other Private/Nationalized Banks?
Yes, loans can be availed against the security of specially minted gold coins sold by Banks weighing maximum up to 50 grams per customer.
15. If I have availed one SBI Personal Gold Loan, can I apply for second loan?
Yes, you can avail maximum 3 gold loans, including the existing one within bank’s prescribed ceiling of Rs 50.00 lacs.
16. Whether NRI customer is eligible for SBI Personal Gold Loan?
Yes, NRI Customer is eligible for loan with his temporary local address.
17. Is there any pre-payment penalty on a SBI Personal Gold Loan?
No, there is no prepayment penalty on SBI Gold Loan.
18. Are there any foreclosure charges on a SBI Personal Gold Loan?
No. Loan can be closed at any point of time without any foreclosure charges.
19. Can I apply for SBI Personal gold loan at any branch?
Yes. The Loan can be applied for at any branch. The loan can also be applied through SBI App “YONO”.
20. What are the methods by which my gold will be verified?
Following methods are used for assaying of gold collateral :
Touch stone method
Nitric acid test
Specific gravity test
Gold Assaying machine
21.  How net weight of Gold collateral is determined?
Answer: The net weight of gold content in collateral is arrived at by excluding all extraneous matters such as wax, string, fastening, precious stones etc. on a liberal scale from gross weight of the Gold collateral during appraisal of Gold for purity and weight.
22. What is the price used to value Gold collateral?
Answer: To value gold collateral, market price as determined by the bank on daily basis is used.
