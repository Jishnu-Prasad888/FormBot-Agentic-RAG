Eligibility
An individual applying for a loan should be at least 18 years old.
The policy has not been issued under the Married Women’s Property Act, 1874.
The policy stands in the name of the borrower only. No third-party Insurance Policy to be admitted as security.
The policy is in force and the insurance premium has been paid upto date.
Eligible security
Loans can be granted against the security of life insurance policies issued by
(a) Life Insurance Corporation of India,
(b) Department of Post Offices/ India Post,
(c) SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd.,
(d) HDFC Standard Life and
(e) ICICI Prudential Life Insurance.
