Premature Withdrawal Conditions
No premature withdrawal is permitted under the scheme for a period of 1 year from the date of opening of FCNR(B) deposit.
In case of premature withdrawal after 1 year and before 3 years, interest will be payable @ 3.50% for the period deposit has run with the Bank.
In case of premature withdrawal between 3 years to less than 5 years, interest will be payable @ 1.00% lower than the rate of interest applicable for the period the deposit has run with the Bank.
Rates are subject to change, without prior notice.
