Objective/ Purpose
•    To provide hassle free finance to our existing Current Account customers for meeting their multiple requirements related to their Business activity.
Features
Target Group:
Current Account customers with Constitution type as individual, Sole Proprietor, Partnership Concerns and classified as Micro/ Small as per definition of MSME
Facilities Available :
Dropline OD.
Quantum of loan :
>₹0.50 lakh to ₹20 Lakhs.
Pricing :
Competitive Pricing Linked to EBR (Linked to Repo Rate and Currently EBR is Repo Rate + 2.65%)
Repayment Period:
Fixed 48 months
Processing Fee/ Upfront Fee:
0.30% of the sanctioned limit + GST.
Other Charges :
As per Bank Guidelines which will be appraised to the borrower before sanction of loans
Collateral Security :
nil.
Special Feature
Simplified journey with minimal Manual Intervention
Pre-Approved/ Pre-Qualified Loans for faster processing
Collateral Free loan upto Rs. 20 Lakhs
Simplified Documentation formalities
Eligibility :
Age of the borrower: Age of Promoter should be maximum 70 years.
