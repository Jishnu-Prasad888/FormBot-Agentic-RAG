Apply for Savings account 24 X 7 on YONO with officially valid documents ( Driving License/Passport/Election Card/ NREGA Card) and one branch visit to complete the KYC process.
Features
Open your Saving Bank Account with single branch visit
Need one of your Officially Valid documents(OVDs) for opening this account
Voter ID card
Driving License
Passport
NREGA JOB Card
Experience 24*7 banking access through Yono app, Internet banking and Mobile banking.
Nomination facility is available.
Eligibility
Resident Indians above 18 years of age who are literate and able to sign uniformly.
Customer is new to SBI.
Officially Valid Document bears the current address of the customer.
PAN is mandatory.
Mobile is mandatory and customer should possess it at the time of opening account. Email address is optional.
How to apply?
Download YONO App
Click New to SBI—Digital Savings Account and then through OVD
Enter your OVD details and other relevant details
Reference Number is generated
Walk into nearest branch and provide ref No
Provide earlier entered OVD copy, sign the required documents and open the account
