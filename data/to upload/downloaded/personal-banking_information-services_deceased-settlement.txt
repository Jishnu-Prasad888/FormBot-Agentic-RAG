Deceased Claim Settlement of Bank Deposits / Safe Deposit Lockers / Safe Custody Articles
Bank has revised deceased claim settlement w.e.f 16.12.2025.
The revised guidelines are issued to provide a harmonized framework and to standardise the documentation for settlement of claims in respect of deposit accounts, safe deposit lockers and articles in safe custody of a deceased customer and to minimise the difficulties faced by the nominees, survivors and legal heirs. However, these revised directions shall not be applicable for Government savings schemes such as Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS), Public Provident Fund (PPF), Mahila Sammana Savings Certificate (MSSC) and Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA).
Claimant(s) can lodge deceased deposit claim in any of his /her choice Branch of SBI and obtain acknowledgment.
In case of Safe Deposit Lockers, claimant(s) has to lodge deceased claim in the branch, where locker was hired by the deceased hirer.
Claimant(s) can lodge deceased claim request for deposit accounts and safe deposit lockers through “Branch Visit” or “Online Mode”. The path for online mode is SBI.BANK.IN >
