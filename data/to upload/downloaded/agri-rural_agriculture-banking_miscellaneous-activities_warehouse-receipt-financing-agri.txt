Purpose
To enable the farmers to avoid distress sale immediately after harvest when the prices are usually low, by providing necessary liquidity support against pledge of these agri. commodities (farm produce).
The loan is given against the receipts issued by
Warehouses registered with WDRA.
Warehouses managed by approved Collateral Managers
Features
Eligible:
Individual farmers(single/joint), FPOs, SHGs, JLGs, Agro-Food processing Units/Farmer Co-operative Societies, PACS.
Type of Facility:
Demand Laon with maximum tenor of 12 months.
Quantum of loan:
Loan Limit (Loan to Value) = 75% of underlying security of Warehouse Receipt (Quantity*Valuation). The commodity offered for pledge will be valued by considering the lowest of the following:
a. Minimum Support price, wherever declared.
b. Current Market price.
c. Value mentioned in e-NWR/WHR.
Limit = (Quantity × Valuation) – Margin.
Maximum loan:
Based on value of value of Warehouse Receipt(s) to be pledged.
For Individual Farmers/SHGs/JLGs: 2 Crores
For FPOs/Farmer Co-operative societies/PACS/Agro-Food Processing units: 10 crores
Moratorium:
NA
Repayment:
The maximum loan term – 12 months. The maximum loan term shall be one month prior to the shelf life of the commodity.
Security:
Primary:
Hypothecation / Pledge of e-NWR / Warehouse Receipts issued by WDRA registered Warehouses / Collaterally Managed Godowns
Collateral:
NIL
Loans given against e-NWRs will be covered in Credit Guarantee Scheme for e-NWR based Pledge Financing (CGSNPF).
Documents
Application form with 2 photos
ID & address proof
eNWRs/WHRs
Land records (in case of farmers)
Security documents for loan.
Any other document as per sanction.
