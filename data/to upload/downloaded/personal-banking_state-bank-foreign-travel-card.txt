How to Get SBI Foreign Travel Card
Who can get SBI Foreign Travel Card
Indian residents traveling abroad(except Nepal and Bhutan).
Professionals/Corporate employees traveling overseas for official purposes.
Students studying abroad (card can be loaded by parents and close relatives as per FEMA guidelines)
Get Your Card from any SBI Branch
Visit any SBI branch, fill out the application form, and submit required documents to get your card instantly.
Apply Online at https://online.sbi.bank.in
You can apply for an SBFTC through RINB (Internet Banking) at https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/.
Path to apply: e-Services → e-Cards → Apply State Bank Foreign Travel Card.
A reference number will be generated. Please take a printout of the online application and, along with the required KYC documents and papers, visit any SBI branch for further processing and issuance of the SBFTC.
