Link your existing SBI Credit Card on YONO SBI once and enjoy the seamless services anytime, anywhere.
Services
Bill Payment
View Card Summary
View Transaction History
Manage PIN
Check & Redeem Reward Points
Block Card
How to link
Log in to YONO app
Visit Cards section and tap on 'My Credit Cards'
Go to 'Do you have a SBI Credit Card?', tap on 'Link Now'
Enter SBI Card username and password or enter card number only
Enter the OTP received on mobile number registered with SBI Card and proceed
Download YONO SBI
