Purpose
Extending finance to traders/owners of goods/manufacturers/processors against Warehouse Receipts issued by Collateral Managers (C.M) with whom we have tie-up (presently NBHC, Star Agri, Sohanlal Commodity Management Private Limited, LTC Commercial Company Pvt Limited and eNWR).
Features : Simplified documentation, Competitive Pricing
Target Group :
Traders/owners of goods/Processors/Manufacturers
Facilities Available :
Cash Credit facility (for limits of Rs.1 cr. and above) / Working Capital Demand Loan and Revolving Demand Loan.
Quantum of loan
Pricing :
Attractive interest rates based on scheme specific rating/external rating or as per extant guidelines of the bank.
EBR (Linked to Repo Rate and Currently EBR is Repo Rate + 2.65%) linked (For MSMEs) & 6 months MCLR linked (Non MSMEs)
Borrower’s Margin / Contribution :
25% to 35% of market price
Repayment Period
Maximum 12 months or expiry date of receipt or when the stock is liquidated, whichever is earlier.
Processing Fee/ Upfront Fee :
Nil upto loan amount of Rs. 1 Cr, thereafter 0.1% p.a (maximum Rs. 3 Lakhs)
Other Charges
Special Feature
Simplified Documentation, attractive pricing.
Eligibility Criteria
Traders/owners of goods/manufacturers for own processing against WHR issued by CM/CWC/SWC.
Security
Primary
- Pledge / Hypothecation of underlying stocks for which WHR has been issued by the Collateral Manager / CWC / SWC, with lien marked in favour of Bank.
Collateral
- Personal Guarantee of partners / directors, wherever applicable.
