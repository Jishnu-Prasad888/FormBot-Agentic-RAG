Purpose
To meet the short-term production / investment credit needs of
The farmers, engaged in agriculture, cultivating own and /or leased land or engaged in cultivation of crops
Farmers in allied activities like Dairy, Poultry, Fisheries, Piggery, Sheep, etc.
Entrepreneurs and famers who needs investment credit for acquiring farm machinery, undertaking land development, irrigation, horticulture, etc.
All other farming activities which are permitted to be classified under agriculture as per RBI/GoI/NABARD guidelines and not covered under the Agri Interest subvention scheme.
Loan Amount
Minimum
: Rs. 5,000/-
Maximum
: Rs. 25,00,000/-
Eligibility
All farmers: Individuals who are owner cultivators, Agri entrepreneurs.
Tenant farmers, Oral lessees & Sharecroppers
Any person engaged in any agriculture or allied activities
Features:
Type of Facility:Demand Loan
Quantum of Loan: Basis of Advance value of gold ornaments per gram of different level of purity (24/22/20/18 carats)
Repayment:12 months from the date of disbursement of the loan.
Security:Pledge of gold ornaments
Documents Required
Two copies of photograph of the borrower
Application
Evidence of land holding or Allied activities.
Any other document as per sanction.
Interest, Fees and Charges
Interest Rate: One Year MCLR rate.
Processing/ Inspection Charges
Loan up to Rs.3.00 lakhs : Nil
Above Rs.3.00 lakhs: 0.30% of loan limit + GST.
For more details, please visit nearest SBI Branch.
