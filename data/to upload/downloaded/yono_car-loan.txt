Car loan on YONO is envisaged as one-stop solution for applying for loan, Covering existing Bank customers (salaried and non salaried customer categories) under self service mode.
Features
Instant in-principle loan approval
Financing up to 100% of 'On-road Price'
Competitive Interest Rate
Longest Repayment Tenure (7 years)
No prepayment charges
Eligibility
For resident individual aged 21 to 70 years
For Salaried customers - Net Annual Income of applicant and/or co-applicant if any, together should be a minimum of Rs. 3,00,000/-.
For Self-Employed customers- Net Profit or Gross Taxable income of Rs. 3, 00,000/- p.a. (income of co-applicant can be clubbed together).
How to Avail
Step1: Login to YONO & under Hamburger menu (☰ ) on top left end > Loan > Car Loan, tap on Apply Now.
Step2: Review and update your personal & employment details
Step3: Select the vehicle details and enter price details
Step4: Select loan amount, tenure and branch
Step5: Accept terms and conditions. Lead and in-principle offer will be generated. Visit your selected branch for further process.
