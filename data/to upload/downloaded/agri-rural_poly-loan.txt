Purpose:
Term loan to meet the cost of construction of polyhouse and purchase of required machinery/ equipment, etc.
Features
Type of Facility
: Agriculture Term Loan
Quantum of Loan
:
Minimum – Rs. 1.00 lakh
Maximum – Rs. 5.00 crores
Margin
:
ATL : 15% of the project cost
15% of the project cost
Interest Rate
a) Up to Rs. 50 lakhs: MCLR + 2.00% p.a.
b) For loans above Rs.50 lakhs: As per Bank’s guidelines
Insurance
:Insurance of assets purchased /created out of Bank’s finance for full market value.
Processing Charges
: As per Bank’s extant guidelines for Agri Segment
Moratorium
:
12 Months Maximum (Should be decided because of activity Financed)
Based on the type of activity.
Eligibility
Individuals/ group of progressive farmers/ FPOs/ JLGs/ SHGs having assured irrigation facilities
The location of the farm should be conducive for marketing of the produce.
Security
Primary :
Hypothecation of crops and polyhouse including all machinery /equipment & other assets created out of Bank’s finance.
Collateral :
Up to Rs 1.60 Lakhs: - Nil
Above Rs 1.60 Lakhs: - As per Bank’s guidelines
Repayment
Repayable in 72 months including maximum moratorium of 12 months.
Documents Required
Duly filled in application form
Proof of agricultural land/ cultivation.
