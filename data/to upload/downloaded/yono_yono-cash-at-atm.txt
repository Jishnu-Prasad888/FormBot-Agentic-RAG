Steps to be followed in YONO app
YONO Pre-login:
YONO app Pre-login-> Tap on YONO Cash
YONO Post login:
Login to YONO app -> MPIN/User Id & Password-> Landing Page-> Tap on YONO Cash
Under the “Create Request” tab, select "Withdraw Cash->ATM", choose the account, enter the withdrawal amount, and create a one-time YONO Cash PIN.
Review the transaction details, accept the terms and conditions, and confirm.
Once the request is processed, you’ll receive a transaction reference number via SMS to your registered mobile number.
Steps to be followed at the ATM
Visit the SBI ATM, click on YONO CASH->YONO Cash Withdrawal and enter the transaction reference number received through SMS.
Enter the withdrawal amount (matching the amount initiated in your YONO Cash Request) and YONO Cash PIN you had set.
The transaction is validated, cash is dispensed and the process is completed.
