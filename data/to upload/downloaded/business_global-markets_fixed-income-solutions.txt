Derivative Solutions to hedge your interest rate risks or to profit from anticipated movements
Hedge interest rate risks on your books
Feature & Benefits
Overnight Indexed Swaps (OIS)
Type of interest rate swap where the floating leg of the swap is linked to an overnight index i.e., FBIL Overnight MIBOR
FBIL Overnight MIBOR compounded every day over the payment period for tenors upto (and including) 1 year
Exchange the difference in the accrued interest arrived according to the fixed and floating interest rates at the semi-annual period maturity / final maturity on the notional principal amount
User for Asset-Liability Management, for hedging interest rate risk, for reducing interest cost without sacrificing liquidity
Standard market tenors
Interest Rate Swaptions
Option on Interest Rate Swaps (OIS), wherein the buyer of the Swaption has the right but not the obligation to enter into an INR OIS at the expiry of the Swaption at a predetermined strike rate
On exercising the option, an OIS position equivalent to the notional of the Swaption is entered into with the counterparty
Standard market tenors are defined as 1M, 3M and 12M Swaptions for OIS maturities of 1Y, 2Y and 5Y
Can be gross settled or net settled
Used for purpose of hedging the interest rate risk
