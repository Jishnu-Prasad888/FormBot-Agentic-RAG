Fully digitized loan journey designed for customers maintaining Salary Package account with the Bank
Features
Loans up to ₹35 lakhs.
Can be availed instantly at your convenience on 24*7 basis
Digitization of entire loan documentation processes viz. loan application, loan agreement, stamping and signature at DDE enabled States/ UTs.
Non-DDE: Real-time sanction on YONO, document execution and single click disbursement at selected branch
Instant loan disbursement.
Aadhaar OTP based e-sign of customer.
Step-by-Step Guided e-mail and SMS Communication System
Eligibility
Salaried customers maintaining Salary Package account with the Bank
Minimum Net Monthly Income of Rs. 25000/-
Minimum Loan amount: Rs. 1 lakh
Maximum Loan Amount: Rs 35 lakhs / 24 times NMI
EMI/NMI Ratio upto 65%
How to Avail
Step 1: Log in to YONO, click Loan & click on Personal Loan
Step 2: Enter your PAN, Date of Birth & Name on PAN and Verify e-mail ID.
Step 3: Upload Photo, choose your desired loan amount, tenure, and EMI payment date.
Step 4: Accept the Terms & Conditions and confirm via OTP.
Step5: Execute the Loan documents digitally through Aadhaar based OTP or visit branch for documentation. Your loan amount will be credited to your Savings Bank account
