Rinn Samadhan 2025-26 Scheme for SME & Agri Segment
Scheme Start date : 03.11.2025                                                                                        Scheme end date: 31.03.2026
Sr No
Applicability of Scheme/Eligibility Conditions
1
Eligible Non-Performing Accounts with cut-off date & Amount
a.
Unsecured Sub-Standard Asset Accounts
with limit sanctioned up to Rs.10 lacs in SME and for Agri Segment in specific products, classified as Sub-Standard
on or before 31.12.2024
.
b.
Doubtful, Loss Asset & AUC Accounts
with outstanding upto Rs.20 Lacs classified as Doubtful & Loss Assets
on or before 31.03.2025
.
c. Deceased NPA cases
as on 30.06.2025
with outstanding up to Rs.20 Lacs in SME & Rs.10 lacs in Agri Segment d. Maximum Borrower wise/PAN wise outstanding not to exceed Rs.20Lacs.
Exclusions
: Cases of frauds, Wilful Defaulters, Non Co-operative borrowers, Cases pending in NCLT, Malfeasance, Staff Accounts, Loan against Deposit & Specified Securities, Gold loans, Corporate Guaranteed Accounts & Pool Purchase accounts etc. are not covered under the Scheme.
2
Concessions available to Borrowers
a. Waiver of notional interest from date of NPA, in respect of all eligible accounts.
b. Hair-cut/ Concessions
c. Legal and any other expenses: All such expenses to be waived unless realised before cut-off date.
3
Payment Terms
a. 10% of the OTS amount upfront at the time of submission of application.
b. Additional 10% of the OTS amount with 30 days from the date of sanction of OTS.
c. Balance OTS amount is to be paid within 90 days from the OTS date. d. No refund / adjustment of any amount already paid under any of the OTS Schemes will be allowed.
4
Early Bird Offer
Additional incentive of 5% on OTS amount will be provided to the borrowers who will pay the entire OTS amount in lumpsum along with the upfront amount.
5
Scheme Period
Last date receipt of acceptance along with upfront payment is on or before 31.03.2026.
