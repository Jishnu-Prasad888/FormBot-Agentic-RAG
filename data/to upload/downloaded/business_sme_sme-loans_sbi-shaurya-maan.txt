To provide Working Capital finance to the defense canteens to carry out procurement from Depots and ease their day-to-day operations.
Features
Target Group :
Registered Defence Canteens of Armed Forces having the Registration Number
Facilities Available :
Clean Overdraft Facility
Quantum of loan :
Minimum.: Rs.1.00 lakh.
Maximum: Rs.5.00 Crore
Pricing :
Competitive Interest Rate linked with MCLR of the Bank
Borrower’s Margin / Contribution :
Nil
Repayment Period
On Demand
Processing Fee/ Upfront Fee :
Nil
Other Charges :
As per Bank Guidelines which will be appraised to the borrower before sanction of loans.
Special Feature
Overdraft Facility for Defense Canteens of Armed Forces
