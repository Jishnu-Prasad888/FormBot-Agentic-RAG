State Bank of India – Representative Office, Dubai
The
State Bank of India Representative Office in Dubai
, established in 2008 under a Representative Office license from the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE), serves as a vital bridge connecting Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) in the UAE with SBI’s vast network across India.
As the overseas extension of
India’s largest bank
, our office provides
personalized support, seamless coordination,
and
dedicated services
to help NRIs manage their banking and financial requirements with ease and confidence.
While we do not undertake direct banking transactions, we facilitate a wide range of services including
account-related assistance, loan document execution,
and
customer support
by closely coordinating with SBI branches and processing centers across India. The Dubai Representative Office acts as a single point of contact, committed to enhancing the service experience for our valued NRI customers in the region.
Our Core Services for NRI Customers
At the SBI Representative Office, Dubai, we are committed to providing seamless support to our NRI customers. Our key services include:
•    NRE/NRO Account Assistance
Guidance and attestation of account opening forms and KYC documents, with forwarding to the respective Global NRI Centers in India.
•    Loan Application Support
Acceptance, attestation and forwarding of loan applications to designated branches and processing centers in India.
•    Banking Services Assistance
Help with ATM card requests, online banking registration, cheque book issuance, and fund transfer requests.
•    Loan Document Execution
Facilitation of sanctioned loan document execution, including Home, Car, Education, and Loan Against Property (LAP) facilities.
We aim to simplify your banking needs and serve as a trusted bridge between you and SBI in India.
FAQs – SBI Representative Office, Dubai
General Queries
Can I open an account directly through the Dubai Office?
Yes. You may submit your account opening forms and related documents at our office. We will verify and forward them to the designated NRI Centre or branch in India for processing.
For more information, please refer to the FAQs – NRI section on the SBI NRI website.

Can financial transactions be done at the Dubai Office?
Our office functions solely as a Representative Office and does not handle any financial transactions directly. However, we facilitate and forward customer requests such as fund transfers, repatriations, and deposit placements to the concerned SBI branches in India.
Do you have offices in other Emirates?
Yes. State Bank of India presently operates two Representative Offices in the UAE – one in
Dubai
and another in
Abu Dhabi
.
Is there any service charge for services availed at the Dubai Office?
No. The Representative Office does not levy any service charges. However, standard bank charges as applicable at the home branch will apply for requests such as fund transfers, ATM issuance, or deposit processing.
For detailed information, please refer to the Service Charges section on the SBI NRI website.
Can I collect my Internet Banking credentials from the Dubai Office?
Internet Banking credentials are now shared directly by the NRI Centre or home branch to the customer’s registered email ID. However, customers may submit related requests through our office for onward transmission to the respective branch.
Do you handle deposits or withdrawals?
The Representative Office does not handle cash transactions. However, customers may submit requests for placing deposits or related instructions, which will be verified and forwarded to their home branch in India for processing.
Can I collect my Debit Card from this office?
Debit cards are not issued or delivered through the Representative Office. However, you may submit a request at our office for forwarding to your home branch for delivery to your registered address, whether in India or overseas.
Can I submit my NRI Home Loan application at the Dubai Office?
Yes. You may submit your
home loan application
and the
related documents
at our office. We will verify and forward them to the designated
Home Loan Processing Centre / Branch in India
for further action.
To know more about SBI’s NRI Home Loan products, please visit
https://sbi.bank.in/web/nri/loans.
Are there any attestation charges?
No. Document attestation at our office is provided
free of cost
as a customer facilitation service.
Can you assist with Power of Attorney (POA) documentation?
Our office is
not authorised
to sign or execute Power of Attorney documents. These must be attested through the
Indian Embassy or Consulate
. However, we provide guidance on the procedure and format as per SBI and Indian regulatory requirements.

FAQs – SBI Representative Office, Abu Dhabi
Can I open an NRI account at Abu Dhabi Branch of SBI?
This is a Representative Office of SBI. We assist you in verifying and assisting your documents for opening NRE/NRO accounts and converting your regular Savings Bank accounts to NRO accounts. The forms submitted at this office are forwarded to Global NRI Centers for account opening in your desired branch in India.
What is the difference between NRE and NRO accounts?
NRE account (Non-Resident External) allows NRIs to deposit foreign earnings in India, with balances fully repatriable and tax-free in India. The balances are held in Indian Rupees. Fixed Deposit created from NRE accounts earns non-taxable interest.
NRO accounts (Non-Resident Ordinary) are used by NRIs to manage income earned in India, such as rent, dividends or pension. NRO balances are partially repatriable, and the interest earned is taxable in India. The balances are held in Indian Rupees.
Can we avail Housing loans from SBI Abu Dhabi?
Being a Representative Office, we assist you in connecting with the branches in India, post sanction documentation, verification/attestation of your documents and submission of complied documents/forms to your loan sanctioning branch.
Is there a Personal loan facility available for NRIs?
There are various loans tailor made for NRIS viz. NRI Housing loan, NRI Education loan, NRI Car loan, Overdraft / Demand Loan against your existing fixed deposits.
Can I avail of an Internet Banking facility for my NRE account?
Yes, if you had earlier not availed the facility, you can do so by visiting onlinesbi.sbi.bank.in and register as a new user. The site will prompt you for your CIF no(Customer Identification File No.), registered mobile no, date of birth. You will be authenticated by your existing debit card PIN. If the customer is not in possession of the debit card PIN the application has an option to register through the branch. For assistance, you can contact the Representative Offices in person to submit an application for registering for internet banking facility.
Is YONO working in the U.A.E.?
YONO LITE, Application version of SBI’s internet banking suitable for mobile handsets is available for both resident and non-resident Indians.
Can I get interest certificates from your office?
Yes, Interest Certificates for loans, deposits, statements of accounts are provided to customers on specific request basis.
