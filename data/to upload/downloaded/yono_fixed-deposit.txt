Park your lump sum amount in an SBI Term Deposit and avail of features like guaranteed returns, choice of interest payout, liquidity through OD or premature withdrawal.
Invest today through OnlineSBI or by visiting your branch.
Features
To enable our customers to place long term deposits to earn attractive rate of interest along with a flexibility in choosing the deposit amount in both Mode STDR/TDR/TSFD ( Tax saver fixed Dep cannot be premature before maturity)
Eligibility: Individuals in single and joint account with Mod of operation E or S, f or S, Any or S (Jointly operated not permissible)
Period of deposit: Minimum – 7 days and Maximum - 10 years But for TSFD the minimum period should be 5 years
Minimum Deposit amount Rs. 1000/- at any one instance. ( Under TSFD
Maximum deposit amount Rs.99,99,999/- at any one instance.
Premature penalty up to Rs.5 lacs is waived and above Rs.5 lacs 0.5% penalty on applicable interest rate.
Nomination available in favour of individual only.
DL/OD can be availed 90% on STDR & 75% ON TDR AND On TSFD no OD allowed
Transferability allowed among branches
Rate of interest as applicable to Term Deposits for Public and Senior Citizens
TDS is applicable at prevalent rate. Form 15G/15H can be submitted by the depositor to get exemption from Tax deduction as per Income Tax Rules.
Most important Terms and Conditions
Interest can be paid on monthly intervals on discounted rate.
Interest can also be paid at Calendar quarter basis.
TDS at prevalent Income tax rate is deducted if Form 15G/15H not submitted.
Auto renewal is exercised if maturity instructions are not given.
