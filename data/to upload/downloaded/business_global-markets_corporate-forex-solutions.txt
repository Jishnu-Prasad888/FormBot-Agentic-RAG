Hedge your currency & interest rate risk
Feature & Benefits
Types of Swaps We Offer
:
Interest Rate Swaps (IRS) : Exchange floating rate obligations for fixed rate payments (or vice versa) to manage interest rate volatility. Ideal for loans, bonds or debt refinancing
Currency Swaps : Swap Principal and interest payments in one currency for another to manage cross-border liabilities or investments. Ideal for long-term hedging for foreign currency loans or receivables
Principal-Only Swaps (POS) : Exchange principal amounts in different currencies without swapping interest payouts - Ideal for managing FX risk on future capital obligations
Coupon-Only Swaps (COS) : Swap only the interest payments in different currencies, keeping principal amounts untouched. Ideal for multinational bond or loan structures
