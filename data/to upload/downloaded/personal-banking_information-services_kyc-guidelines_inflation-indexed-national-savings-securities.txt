Inflation Indexed National Savings Securities-cumulative(IINSS-C)
Reserve Bank of India has launched the Inflation Indexed National Savings Securities-Cumulative (IINSS-C) bond which offers the investors a return that's 1.5% more than inflation based on the consumer-price-index. Interest will be compounded half yearly, enhancing effective yield on investments.
The minimum investment limit is Rs. 5,000/- and the maximum limit is Rs. 500,000/- per applicant per annum.
