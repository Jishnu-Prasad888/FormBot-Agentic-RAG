NON-CALLABLE TERM DEPOSIT - RETAIL (NCD-R)
Eligibility:
Resident Individuals and Non- Individual Customers (Minors are excluded, Separate scheme is available for NRI Customers)
Special Category of Customers :
Senior Citizens / Staff/ Staff Senior Citizens are eligible for additional Interest Rate applicable over Non Callable Rates for Public.
(NRI Senior Citizens / NRI Staff not eligible)
Minimum Amount (In multiples of Rs. 1000/- thereafter):
Rs.1,00,01,000/- (Rupee one crore and one thousand) *Separate product for Non-callable Bulk deposit
Maximum Amount:
< (Less than) Rs.3.00 crore
Tenor:
Two tenors:
1 year and 2 years only
Rate of Interest:
30 bps over card rate of TD Retail for tenor 1 year.
40 bps over card rate of TD Retail for tenor 2 years.
Eligible Products
TDR/ STDR only
Not available under any other scheme
Interest Pay out Option
Monthly/ Quarterly / Half yearly/Yearly Interest Pay-out is available for TDR.
For STDR, Interest shall be paid on maturity along with principal.
Premature Withdrawal:
Strictly not allowed
except in case of:
If directed by Court/ Regulator or in case of Winding up/ Bankruptcy
Death of Individual Depositor(s)
Right to set off
Loan Facility:
Demand Loan facility available and
No Overdraft facility allowed.
TDS Application:
Applicable
Available through Channel:
The product shall be available through Branch CBS channel only.
