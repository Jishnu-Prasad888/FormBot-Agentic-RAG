Trade with margins as low as 25% and carry forward position up to 30 days using cash or collateral to avail required margin. Customer can choose to convert to delivery and get the stocks in demat account or square off any time before expiry.
Key Features
Lower margin
Option to convert to delivery/square-off
Carry your position
Margin can be in form of cash/stocks
How to avail
Login to Trading account through SBI Securities web platform
Go to order placement (Buy/Sell) menu
Select product type as E-Margin while placing order
