How to buy?
Log onto YONO SBI app
Tap the SBI Life - eShield Insta banner, click Or Tap on Insurance>Buy a Policy>Life>Term Plan>SBI LifeeShield Insta
Select/Enter Personal Details & Tap on ‘Continue’
Choose Sum Assured & select from 2 plan options along with Premium Frequency & Tap on Continue
Enter Health Details & Tap on ‘Continue’
Enter Nominee Details/ Other Details & Tap on ‘Continue’
Click & Upload your selfie & Tap on ‘Next’
Review & Verify details and Tap on ‘Continue’
Select your account for premium payment, verify with OTP, and confirm.
Download YONO SBI
