Highlights of Experts committee on RBI’s Resolution framework for COVID related stress
Purpose :
Resolution framework for Borrowers under COVID-19 related stress
Features
Invocation Date :
May be invoked not later than Dec 31, 2020
Resolution plan (RP) needs to be implemented within 180 days from the date of invocation
Signing of ICA and Provision requirement :
Invoked once lenders representing 75% by value and 60% by number agree.
Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA) to be signed by all lenders within 30 days of invocation.
Lenders who have not signed ICA, to make a provision higher of 20% or as per IRAC norms, upon expiry of 30 days from invocation
Key guidelines :
Residual tenor of loan may be extended by max 2 years with or without payment moratorium.
Asset classification may be maintained as standard or upgraded to standard subject to the RP
Conversion of loans into securities :
To include restructuring / regularization / change in ownership
May provide for conversion of debt into equity or other marketable non-convertible debt securities provided amortization and coupon are similar to terms of debt
Post implementation :
In respect of exposures, any default by borrower with any of the signatories to the ICA during the monitoring period shall trigger a Review Period of 30 days
Eligibility
Eligibility :
Only for those borrowers having stress due to COVID and which were classified as standard and with arrears less than 30 days as at March 1, 2020
