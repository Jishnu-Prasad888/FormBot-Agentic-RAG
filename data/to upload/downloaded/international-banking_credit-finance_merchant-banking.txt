ECBs & Syndications
With presence across all major global finance hubs, SBI aims to meet foreign currency borrowing requirement of India based corporates. Funding can be arranged in multiple currencies including USD, EURO, GBP and Yen.
SBI underwrites and syndicates foreign currency loans apart from advising on various aspects of foreign currency borrowings, including structuring, syndication and hedging.
Funds are arranged by SBI on a bilateral basis or through syndication.
Key Features of our offering:
Funding foreign currency requirement of
Indian corporates as permitted under RBI’s ECB guidelines.
Overseas acquisition and capital expenditure needs of Indian Corporates.
General corporate purpose requirement of overseas subsidiaries/ JVs with recourse to Indian Parent.
No Withholding Tax for Indian corporates.
Hedging solutions provided for Foreign Currency Loans.
Non-fund-based solutions (Bank Guarantees, Letter of Credit etc) for execution of overseas projects of India based entities.
Key benefits to Corporates:
The cost of borrowing is usually cheaper in foreign currency, which can be tapped by Indian Corporates through ECB.
ECBs on a completely hedged basis can be competitive compared to INR borrowing; if natural hedge is available, then the differential rate can be substantial. Avenues of lower cost funds will improve the profitability of the companies.
The borrower can diversify the investor base when raised through syndication.
It provides access to international markets for the borrowers and gives global recognition to borrower.
