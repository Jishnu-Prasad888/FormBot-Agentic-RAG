About SBI NMRC City1 Card
State Bank of India and Noida Metro Rail Corporation (NMRC) Ltd. introduces to you CITY1 Card.
State Bank NMRC CITY1 Card, is a contactless dual interface (EMV chip based) prepaid card with stored value functionality for providing a safe, secure and seamless way of payment. Apart from a simple way to pay fares in the Noida – Greater Noida Metro, this card offers extended usage in Noida Metro buses as well. Compartmentalization of amount for different utilities such as transit amongst others is a unique feature of this State Bank NMRC CITY1 Card, which can be used for purchase of tickets or loading of passes for metro as well as buses, using the store value of the card.
State Bank NMRC CITY1 Card offers many exciting features and a host of benefits for all your payment needs within Noida
Cashless travels in Noida-Greater Noida Metro
Purchase tickets and store passes for your Metro travel needs.
Purchase tickets for the Noida buses using the same card
Chip-protection to ensure better security for your card
Easy to use Tap-and-Pay functionality

FAQ
Q. What is SBI – NMRC CITY1 card?
Ans.
This is a State Bank of India and Noida Metro Rail Corporation (NMRC) co-branded prepaid card, having a contactless dual interface (EMV chip), with stored value functionality for making Tap-and-Go payments at Noida and Greater Noida Metro Stations.
Q. What is MTS card?
Ans.
Mass Transit Systems (MTS) card is a specific category of Prepaid card enabled only for payments across various modes of public transport such as metro, buses, rail, & waterways, tolls and parking. These Cards can be issued without KYC verification of the holders. MTS cards will be restricted to Transit use-cases only, operating in offline transaction mode and the amount outstanding, in such Cards shall not exceed Rs. 3,000/- at any point of time.
Note: Threshold limit for Chip/Global Balance on the card is a maximum of Rs. 2000/- only.
Q. How can I get CITY1 Card?
Ans  (
i
) To get Minimum Details City1 Card you just have to follow the below mentioned simple steps:
Visit your nearest Noida Metro station and request for the City 1 Minimum Details Card at the Ticket Counter.
Fill your details in the simple Application Form and submit it to the operator.
After entering the details by the operator, One Time Pin (OTP) will be generated and sent to your mobile number provided in the application.
After OTP validation, pay the top-up amount to load balance on the card for usage.
Even you can fill required details in the webpage https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in/web/#/apply-card & note down reference number.
Give the reference number to ticket operator to get your City1 Card.
(ii) To get Mass Transit System (MTS) City1 Card you just have to follow the below mentioned simple steps:
Visit your nearest Noida Metro station and request for the City1 MTS Card at the Ticket Counter.
Provide your Mobile number to the operator.
After entering the Mobile number by the operator, One Time Pin (OTP) will be generated and sent to your Mobile number.
After OTP validation, pay the top-up amount to load balance on the card for usage.

What should I do if my card is Stolen, Lost or Damaged?
Ans. If your card is lost, stolen or damaged, please follow the below mentioned steps:
Customers can block the card on Customer portal i.e https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in wherein customer can login using his/her credentials.
Report the information immediately at TOM Counter/Customer Portal or SBI Contact Centre 1800-1234 (toll free) or landline number 080-26599990 (toll) 24x7 and report the loss of card. The contact center executive will ask for identification details, e.g. Card Number, Mobile Number, date of birth, etc. On receipt of the information, your card will be blocked to ensure that it is not being used fraudulently by any unauthorized person. A ticket number will be sent to your registered mobile number after blocking the card.
Visit your nearest Noida Metro station for Card Replacement and submit a simple request letter for the same. After ensuring that your previous card has been blocked, a fresh replacement card will be issued to you.
