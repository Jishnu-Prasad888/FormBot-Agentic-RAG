Power Jyoti Current Account is an exclusive solution for any institution desirous of collecting funds from various remitters through our core banking Branches or Internet Banking with Pre Uploaded Data . Power Jyoti Current account is highly preferable by Universities , Colleges, Schools and other Government Bodies to collect fees /funds/ fines etc. Business Firms also appreciate Power Jyoti PUL Current Account for collecting funds from Dealers , Sub Dealer , Sales Outlets etc. through any of our branches. Detailed MIS is also generated in Power Jyoti PUL Current Account.
