Customers can avail instant overdraft facility, against the mutual fund units of all AMCs registered with CAMS
Features
Loan available against MF schemes of all AMCs registered with CAMS
Minimum Loan Amount: Rs 25,000/-
Maximum Loan Amount:
Equity/ Hybrid/ ETF MF: Rs. 10 lakhs
Debt/ FMP MF: Rs. 5 crores
Margin:
Equity/ Hybrid/ ETF MF: 50%
Debt/ FMP MF: 25%
Interest Rate:
Click here
to view the interest rates
Eligibility
Resident Indians over 18 years of age having SB/CA account in single name with SBI
MF holding with CAMS should be in single name only
Bank and MF account should have same PAN and mobile number.
Eligible open-ended MF Schemes of all AMCs registered with CAMS.
How to Avail
After Login to YONO, under Hamberger menu > Loan > Loan Against Mutual Fund Units, click on Apply Now.
Review the product features, eligibility details and click on “I am interested
Select “Equity” or “Debt” fund and enter requisite loan amount
Select amount of lien to be marked against desired funds
Review processing fee, branch and lien marking details
Accept Terms & Conditions, enter OTP & confirm Loan account will be created, and Overdraft limit will be enabled
