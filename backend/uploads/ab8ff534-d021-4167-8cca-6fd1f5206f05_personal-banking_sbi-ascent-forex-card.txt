How to Get SBI Ascent Forex Card
Who can get SBI Foreign Travel Card
Indian resident students travelling abroad for higher studies (except Nepal and Bhutan)
Students pursuing education overseas, whose Forex Card can be loaded by parents or close relatives as per FEMA guidelines

Required Documents for SBI Ascent Forex Card
Signed application form (Click here to download)
Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
PAN Card
Proof of travel
Visa / Air ticket / Admission letter
Proof of residence (if address differs from passport)
Note
: If your address is different from the one mentioned in your passport, please carry valid proof of residence.
