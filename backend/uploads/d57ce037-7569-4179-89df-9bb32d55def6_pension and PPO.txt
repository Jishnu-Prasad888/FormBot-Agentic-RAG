PPO Number: What is PPO? How Does PPO Work?
What is PPO?
When it comes to saving for retirement, the Employee Pension Scheme (EPS) has been a popular choice since its launch in 1995. The EPS is a social security scheme in India that is part of the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) scheme. It primarily benefits employees in the organised sector and provides them with a pension after retirement. One essential element within the EPS is the Pension Payment Order, also known as PPO. Many people mistake EPS and PPO to be the same thing. To clear up this confusion and help you make better decisions about your retirement funds, it is essential to take a closer look at PPO's meaning in pension and how it works.

PPO full form in pension is Pension Payment Order. It is a unique reference code assigned to every pensioner under the EPS. This distinctive twelve-digit code holds paramount importance as it allows you to access your pension-related information online seamlessly. It can also be used to address any concerns you may have about your pension. Additionally, it can be used to monitor the progress of your pension application and manage your accounts at the Central Pension Account Office (CPAO).

What is a PPO Number?
The PPO number is the same as the PPO. It is an alphanumeric figure, with each segment holding specific information, as explained below:

First five digits: These signify the code number of the PPO issuing authority.
Next two digits: These indicate the year when the PPO was issued.
Next four digits: These show the sequential number of the PPO.
Last digit: These work as a digital check digit to improve the security and accuracy of the PPO.
How to Check PPO Number?
Below are the steps to check the PPO number:

Step 1: Visit the official website of the Employee Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) at www.epfindia.gov.in.
Step 2: Click on the 'Services' tab and select 'For Employees.'
Step 3: Find and click on the 'Pensioners' Portal' link under online services.
Step 4: Select 'Know your PPO number' and provide either your bank account number or the EPF account number.
After you submit these details, your PPO number will be shown on the webpage.

How Does PPO Work?
The PPO functions as a comprehensive tool that not only facilitates the smooth processing of your pension benefits but also becomes indispensable in various other scenarios. The PPO plays a pivotal role in your life post-retirement, which is why it is important to understand how it works.

The PPO can offer several uses, as highlighted below:

Provides comprehensive information: The PPO number features crucial details about retirement, including the pensioner's information, pension amount, commencement date, and disbursing authority. Regularly checking your PPO status is essential for a seamless retirement.
Monitor pension with PPO Number: With your unique PPO number, you can receive and monitor your pension efficiently. It helps you track your money and identify any delays in receiving your pension.
Grievance resolution: When dealing with any concerns or grievances with the EPFO, you can use your PPO number and submit a grievance. The PPO number is linked to any of your queries and issues to ensure prompt redressal.
Bank account updates: If you switch to a new bank account for any reason, you need to provide the bank with your PPO number. It is mandatory to provide the new bank with your PPO information for a smooth transition and to ensure you continue to receive your pension.
life certificate: Providing the PPO number is a prerequisite while submitting the annual life certificate. This certificate is essential to ensure the continuity of your pension.

What is a Pension Payment Order?

The PPO is an essential component of pensions. It serves as a unique reference code for every pensioner under EPS. This twelve-digit alphanumeric code plays a crucial role in providing seamless access to online pension-related information, addressing concerns, monitoring application progress, and managing accounts at the CPAO. The PPO number has distinct segments and holds specific information, as highlighted below:

The first five digits signify the code number of the PPO issuing authority.
The next two digits indicate the year of PPO issuance.
The next four digits represent the sequential number of the PPO.
The last digit functions as a digital check digit to enhance security.

How to check the PPO number?

You can follow these steps to discover your PPO number:

Step 1: Visit the official website of EPFO at www.epfindia.gov.in.
Step 2: Scroll to 'Online Services' and click on the 'Pensioner's Portal'.
Step 3: Navigate to 'Know your PPO number' on the right side of the Pensioner's portal.
Step 4: Fill in your bank account number or PF number.
Step 5: After entering the details, you will receive your PPO number, along with your Member ID and pension type.

What do the first five digits signify in a PPO number?

The initial five digits in a PPO number signify the code number of the PPO issuing authority.

Is PPO mandatory if I need to transfer my PF account from one bank branch to another?

Yes, a PPO number is mandatory for receiving an EPS pension after transferring your PF account from one bank branch to another. The new bank will demand this information and will likely not process your pension until you submit it. Therefore, you must provide the new bank with the PPO number as soon as you can to ensure a smooth and quick transition of your pension benefits.

To get pension from State Bank of India, the retiring employee has to start the process at least 6 months prior to retirement.

In the process the employee has to open account with any branch of State Bank of India, if required with spouse (which is permitted now) and has to provide the account number to the Department from which he/she retires.

He/she has to fill up the account details in the pension papers. While opening account the retiree has to give his/her PAN number, mobile number and email_id (if available).PAN No. will enable correct accounting of the Pensioner's TDS.

After completion of data entry at CPPC, pension shall be credited to the identified account within a period of 3-4 days.

In November month every year the pensioner is required to submit his/ her life certificate to enable the Bank to pay pension continuously.

Critical Illness Cover - The Policy has got one more optional cover for Critical Illness for under noted 14 ailments, for Sum 
Insured of Rs. 5 lacs for the pensioners with completed age below 65 years as on 15.01.2023. However, those who had opted for the 
Critical Illness Plan in expired policy would continue to renew the plan beyond 65 yrs. Those who are already the members of the 
Critical Illness Plan in the expired Policy would continue to renew their Critical Illness Plan even beyond the age of 65 yrs. 
1. Stroke resulting in permanent symptoms - Brain stroke occurs when blood flow to an area in the brain is cut off. This
can lead to permanent brain damage and paralysis. The patient might be restricted to bed throughout his life. 
2. Cancer of specified severity - Refers to a malignant tumour with uncontrolled growth and spread of malignant cells
accompanied by invasion & destruction of normal tissues.
3. Kidney failure requiring regular dialysis - When kidney function deteriorates beyond a certain point, it can no longer
filter blood. Patient needs regular dialysis throughout his life, where a machine performs the function of a kidney. 
4. Major organ/bone marrow transplant - When any of the organs like the heart, liver, kidney or the bone marrow fail, they need
to be replaced for the person to live. Transplantation is the process of replacing the failed organ with another healthy one
given by a healthy donor.
5. Multiple sclerosis with persisting symptoms - In multiple sclerosis (MS), the central nervous system is affected. The immune
system attacks the protective cover of nerves causing problems with basic body functions. MS does not have a cure till now
and it can create a seriously disabling condition for life.
6. Open chest CABG (Coronary Artery Surgery) - When coronary arteries are obstructed, blood flow to the heart decreases
leading to danger of heart attack. Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG) is done to improve blood flow through a major
surgery.
7. First Heart Attack - If the major artery to the heart gets blocked, oxygen-rich blood supply to the heart is disrupted and a person
gets his first heart attack. To prevent further heart attacks and consequent death, surgery needs to be performed.
8. Coma of specified severity-A state of unconsciousness where the patient does not respond to any external stimuli or
internal need. He has to be kept on life support system.
9. Heart valve replacements -When an aortic valve fails to open properly, it needs to be replaced with a biological or
mechanical valve through a surgery, to ensure proper blood flow.
10. Permanent paralysis of limbs - Due to damage to the nervous system, one or more limbs might lose their functionality for life.
11. Motor neuron disease with permanent symptoms - This is a disorder that selectively affects motor neurons, and the
person loses control over the voluntary muscles of his body. This disease worsens overtime and cannot be cured.
12. Aorta Graft Surgery - When the main artery leaving the heart, known as aorta, is diseased, surgery is performed to remove the
diseased portion, replacing it with synthetic graft. The aorta is the main passage for oxygen-rich blood to flow from heart to
other major organs of the body, like the brain, etc.
13. Total Blindness - A person loses total vision due to some condition or disease like glaucoma, cataract, diabetes, etc. In case of
total blindness, the condition is permanent and irreversible.
14. Open heart replacement or repair of heart valves-Due to defect or disease in the cardiac valve, it has to be repaired or replaced
by open heart valve surgery. Each of the above critical illness conditions has to be diagnosed and confirmed by a specialist
medical practitioner. The detailed Policy terms & conditions may be referred to for complete definition of the above 14 listed
ailments and conditions under which the claim would be admissible.
Critical Illness Cover will not be available separately and can be taken only in conjunction of Base Plan and Super Top-up Plan. Other
