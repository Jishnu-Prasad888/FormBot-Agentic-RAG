Financing to JLGs for Sustainable Livelihoods
To augment flow of credit to Micro Entrepreneurs /artisans / individuals in Non-Farm Sector activities and Allied Agri activities
For financing to generate employment opportunities through setting up of new/scaling of existing Self Employment ventures/projects/micro units as per State Specific Schemes
Eligibility Criteria
Members should be of similar gender, socio-economic status, and background carrying out similar activities and who agree to function as a Joint Liability Group.
The members should be engaged in non-farm activities or Allied Agri activities within the area of operation of the Branch.
All other criteria as per RBI -Regulatory Framework of Microfinance
