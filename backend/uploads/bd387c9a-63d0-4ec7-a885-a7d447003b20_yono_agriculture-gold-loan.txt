Apply for Agriculture Gold Loans 24X7 on YONO and one branch visit to complete the loan process.
Purpose
To meet the short-term production/ investment credit needs of
The farmers, engaged in agriculture, cultivating own and/or leased land or engaged in cultivation of crops.
Farmers in allied activities like Dairy, Poultry, Fisheries, Piggery, Sheep etc.
Entrepreneurs and farmers who needs investment credit for acquiring farm machinery, undertaking land development, irrigation, horticulture, transportation of Agri product etc.
All other farming activities which are permitted to be classified under agriculture as per RBI/GoI/NABARD guidelines.
Eligibility
All farmers : Individuals who are owner cultivators, Agri entrepreneurs.
Tenant farmers, Oral lessees & Sharecroppers
Any person engaged in any agriculture or allied activities and want to repay loans availed from non-institutional lenders as well as persons engaged in activities permitted by RBI to be classified under agriculture. A self-declaration has to be obtained from the applicant that he/she is engaged in Agri and allied activities and that the loan availed against the pledge of Gold ornaments is for repayment of higher interest rate loans availed from non-institutional lenders.
