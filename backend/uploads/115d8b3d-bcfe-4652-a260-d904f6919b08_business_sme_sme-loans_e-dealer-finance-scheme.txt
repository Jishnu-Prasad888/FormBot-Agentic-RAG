Purpose
To provide hassle free finance to authorised exclusive Dealers/stockiest/distributors/franchisees (hereinafter called “dealers”) of Industry Majors (IMs) for purchase of Inventory
Features
Target Group :
authorised Dealers/stockiest/distributors/franchisees
Facilities Available :
Cash Credit
Quantum of loan
Need based
Past Performance or Projected Sales, whichever is higher or
Limit Recommended by IM, whichever is Lower.
Pricing :
Competitive Interest Rate Linked to EBR(Repo rate+2.65%) (For MSME Borrowers)/MCLR(Non MSME Borrowers)
Borrower’s Margin / Contribution :
Nil
Repayment Period
Credit period up to 90 days. Yearly renewal
Processing Fee/ Upfront Fee :
Minimum single unified charges comprising of Processing Fee, Inspection Charges, Equitable Mortgages Charges, and facility fee ranging from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 30,000/-
Other Charges
Nil
Special Feature :
The transactions take place on a web based platform without branch intervention
Eligibility Criteria
All Authorised dealers of Industry Majors with whom the Bank has tie-up arrangement
Other Conditions
Transactions are done on Internet Banking Platform of the bank (no cheque book required) & which results in easy, convenient & hassle free banking facilities to all stake holders i.e. Bank, IM & Dealer.
Multiple Channel Repayment Option such as Cash, Cheque, Fund Transfer, NEFT, RTGS etc.
Transaction through 2 methods i.e. PULL METHOD (When the Industry Major makes a request for debit in the dealer’s account) & PUSH MODEL (When the dealer originates the remittance)
