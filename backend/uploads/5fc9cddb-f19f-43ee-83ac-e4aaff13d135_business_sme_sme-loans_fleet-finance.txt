Pricing :
Competitive pricing linked to EBR (Linked to Repo Rate and Currently EBR is Repo Rate + 2.65%) / 6 Months MCLR
Repayment Period:
Scores between 50% to less than 60%: Max 60 months (including moratorium of up to 2 months).
Scores 60% and above: Max 66 months (including moratorium of up to 2 months)..
Repayment in EMI basis. Negotiated repayment can be done depending on cash accruals.
Upfront Fee
: upto 1.50% of the loan amount based on internal rating of the Unit.
Other Charges
: Annual Review of Term Loan: 0.05% of the sanctioned loan amount
Collateral Security
: Loans should be covered under CGTMSE. Guarantee fee to be borne by the borrower. For loans which are not eligible for CGTMSE Coverage, should be covered by tangible collateral security of >=25%.
