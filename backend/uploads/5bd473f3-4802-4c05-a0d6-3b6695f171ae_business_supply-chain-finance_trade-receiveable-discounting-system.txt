Purpose
To help MSMEs unlock the funds lying in trade receivables at competitive interest rate RBI has come up with the concept of Receivables Exchange called TReDS platform. TReDS Platform (Trade Receivable Discounting System) is an institutional mechanism set up for financing of trade receivables of MSMEs from corporates and other buyers including Government Departments and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) through multiple financiers.
RBI has issued license to four entities to operate TReDS platform in India, namely Receivables Exchange of India Limited (RXIL), Mynd Online National Exchange (M1) ,Invoicemart (A.TReDS) and C2FO.
Eligible Participants:
Buyers
: Buys goods from sellers and has to pay at the end of the credit period
Sellers
: Provides goods and services to large buyers and raises the invoice on the buyer for payment after credit period.
Financiers
: Buys seller’s invoices and assumes ownership of receivables to be paid by buyer on due date.
Exchange Platform
: Entities operating TReDS Platforms in India
Key Benefits to participants:
a. Key Benefits to all participants
Easy and quick access to all the funds.
End-to-end digital platform and payments
Competitive discount rates through an auction mechanism
System driven and transparent.
Paperless and hassle-free process
Minimal administrative cost
Added benefits to existing SBI customers.
b. Benefits to Buyers:
Efficient risk management.
Reduced operational cost.
c. Benefits to Sellers:
Timely payment of receivables
Efficient risk management.
Reduced operational cost.
Better liquidity management.
