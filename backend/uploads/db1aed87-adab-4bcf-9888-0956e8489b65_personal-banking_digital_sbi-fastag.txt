What is SBI FASTag.
SBI FASTag is a device that employs Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology for making toll payments directly from the prepaid or savings account linked to it. It is affixed on the windscreen of your vehicle and enables you to drive through toll plazas, without stopping for cash transactions. The tag can be purchased from Tag issuers and if it is linked to the prepaid account, then you need to recharge/ top up the tag as per your requirement.

2) What is a FASTag?
FASTag is a device that employs Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology for making toll payments directly while the vehicle is in motion. FASTag (RFID Tag) is affixed on the windscreen of the vehicle and enables a customer to make the toll payments. FASTag is operational at all National Highway toll plazas and selective State toll plazas in India as per IHMCL approved list.

4) Is FASTag mandatory for all vehicles in India?
Yes. As per amendments in the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR) 1989 by the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, FASTag is mandatory for all class M & N vehicles (four wheelers and above, carrying goods and/or passengers) w.e.f. 1st January 2021.
5) Is FASTag mandatory for new motor vehicle insurance?
Yes. As per amendments in the CMVR 1989 by the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, FASTag has been made mandatory while getting a new 3rd party insurance w.e.f. 1 April 2021.
6) Who is implementing FASTag program?
FASTag is a flagship programme of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways & NHAI. Indian Highways Management Company Limited (IHMCL), a company promoted by NHAI has been mandated to implement FASTag programme in India.
National Payments Corporation of India has been engaged to provide Clearing & Settlement House (CCH) services for this FASTag Programme.
7) What is KYV process?
KYV stands for Know Your vehicle in FASTag parlance. As per NPCI & IHMCL guidelines, customer need to upload KYV images and bank representative will approve or reject the same. Customer can upload the images again on rejection and it can be allowed for maximum five times and after that tag will be marked as closed.

10) What will happen to KYV “non-compliant” FASTag?
FASTag cannot be used at toll plazas or any other places as tag will be put into hotlist. Customer is given leverage of three days to get the KYV process completed from date of registration. If it is not completed, then tag will be put to hotlist.
11) What is the FASTag Annual Pass?
Annual Pass activated on the FASTag allows free passage of Private Car/Jeep/Van at designated National Highway (NH) and National Expressway (NE) fee plazas for one year or 200 crossings (whichever is earlier). The Annual Pass will be effective from 15th August 2025.
12) How will Annual Pass be activated?
Annual Pass can be purchased from Rajmargyatra mobile application or NHAI website. Annual Pass will be activated after verifying the eligibility of the vehicle and the associated FASTag by Rajmargyatra. Upon successful verification, the user must make a payment of ₹3,000 through the Rajmargyatra mobile application or NHAI website. Once the payment is confirmed, the Annual Pass will be activated on the registered FASTag within the time defined by IHMCL.
13) If I already have a FASTag, do I need to buy a new one for the Annual Pass?
No. You do not need to purchase a new FASTag. The Annual Pass can be activated on your existing FASTag, provided it meets the eligibility criteria (i.e., it is properly affixed on the vehicle’s windshield, linked to a valid Vehicle Registration Number, not blacklisted etc).
14) I already have a Local Pass for the toll plaza near my home. Can I still activate the Annual Pass? Will crossings be deducted there?
Yes. You can activate the Annual Pass even if you have a Local/Monthly Pass. At toll plazas where another local/monthly pass is active, crossings will not be counted from your Annual Pass. NPCI has created separate code for Annual Pass, and this will be consumed by only NH/NE fee plazas. Non-NH/NE plazas will not consume this code and will behave as per existing tolling standards.
15) What is the meaning of tag getting hostlisted/blacklisted? What can be done to avoid the hotlist/blacklist of tag?
A hotlisted tag means the tag which has been blocked by the issuer bank and cannot be used to pay toll at a toll plaza. A hotlisted tag can be removed from hotlist category after complying with the requirement of the issuer bank.
A blacklisted tag means tag has been used for violation of guidelines and on specific complaints received from various government agencies like IHMCL,NPCI,RBI,Central/State government agencies, police authorities, etc.
There are various reasons of hotlisting/blacklisting a tag. In such cases customer may contact to
helpdesk.fastag@sbi.co.in
mail id and get the reason of hotlist. To avoid hotlisting of tag, customer should recharge his / her tag when he/she gets the alert of low balance from his/her bank.
Also, FASTag may get hotlisted/blacklisted in case of any violation such as vehicle – tag class mismatch, non-affixation on the windscreen, KYV not completed, KYC expired and tag carrying in hand, etc.

27) Do I need to use any specific lane at the toll plaza for FASTag?
Only vehicles fitted with FASTag are allowed to enter FASTag lanes of NH toll plazas. MoRTH has already directed to convert all lanes in each direction as FASTag lane. A vehicle without valid FASTag entering FASTag lane will be charged double the applicable toll amount in cash.

30) In case of harassment/ misconduct/ discourteous/ rude behavior of toll collection staff, what should we do?
In case of such a scenario, a complaint may be lodged with respective Project Directors at the toll plaza. Further, the incident may be reported at etc.nodal@ihmcl.com or call on toll free number 1033.
31) Can one vehicle use more than one FASTag
Under one vehicle one tag policy of IHMCL/NHAI, one vehicle is assigned with only one FASTag issued by a bank. However, under circumstances, if the vehicle user is not satisfied with the service of a bank, he/she can get new FASTag assigned from other Bank provided that the earlier tag has been closed by the bank.
32) Can a FASTag bought for my one vehicle be used for another vehicle?
No, as per guidelines FASTag is issued to each vehicle based on RC copy of the vehicle. If there is mismatch, the ETC system at the toll plaza will detect the class mismatch and the FASTag is liable to be ‘blacklisted’ by the issuer bank and no new FASTag can be issued further against that vehicle number. FASTag users are advised to desist from such practice.

34) Can FASTag be used at places other than National highways?
FASTag system provides a unified and interoperable Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) solution. Currently, FASTag is operational at selective state toll plazas and all national highway toll plazas across India.
Different use cases like parking, fuel payment, etc. are being enabled as and when guidelines are received from IHMCL/NPCI.
