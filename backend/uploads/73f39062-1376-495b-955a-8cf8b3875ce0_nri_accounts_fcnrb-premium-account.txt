Features & Benefits of FCNR(B) Premium Account
Get a combined benefit of a FCNR (B) deposit with Forward Cover, which results in the higher yield.
FCNR (B) deposit tenor: 1 year and up to 5 years
Forward contract tenor: 1 year and up to 5 years
FCNR(B) deposit will be placed in 6 Currencies (USD,GBP,EUR,CAD,AUD & JPY)
Minimum deposit:
10,000 USD/GBP/EUR/CAD/AUD Currencies .
1,000,000 JPY
Forward contract can be booked for final return (Cross Currency) in INR, USD,GBP, EURO, CAD, AUD or JPY.
No charges for booking forward contract.
In case of cancellation of Forward Contract by the depositor, cancellation charges and exchnage loss if any, has to borne by the depositor at prevailing rate.
If FCNR (B) deposit is closed before maturity, no penalty will be levied for premature withdrawal and applicable rate of interest will be as per terms and conditions governing FCNR (B) deposits.
