ATM / ADWM Features
All our ATMs and ADWMs are EMV Compliant, capable of processing the transactions through the highly secured EMV chip of the cards.
ADWMs (Automated Deposit cum Withdrawal Machines OR Cash Recyclers) have the dual functionality, i.e., to deposit cash and to withdraw cash.
For prevention of un-authorized transactions, 4-digits OTP (One-Time Password) is sent on the registered mobile number of SBI Customers for all transactions of above Rs.10,000/-, done at SBI ATMs / ADWMs using SBI Debit Cards.
SBI Customers can transact in English / Hindi / Regional Languages. Currently, 11 Regional Languages are supported (Assamese, Bengali, Oriya, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Urdu) which are displayed based on the location of the ATM / ADWM.
SBI ATMs / ADWMs support Voice Guidance feature (“Talking ATMs”) as per IBA Guidelines.
All our SBI ATMs and ADWMs support card-based and card-less transactions.
Services available:
Debit Card based Cash Withdrawal:
The most popular service on our ATMs and ADWMs, enables customers to withdraw cash up to the daily limit of SBI Debit Card. Please refer the
Cards
section for daily cash withdrawal limits for all SBI Debit Cards variants. Other Banks’ cardholders can withdraw upto Rs.10,000/- PER TRANSACTION at SBI ATMs and ADWMs (Cash Recycler). Similarly, SBI Debit cardholders can withdraw upto Rs.10,000/- PER TRANSACTION at other Banks’ ATMs and ADWMs (Cash Recycler).
YONO CASH (Card-less cash withdrawal) :
SBI offers FREE card-less cash withdrawal for its customers through the Bank’s YONO Apps (YONO and YONO Lite). SBI customers can initiate the cash withdrawal transaction through the YONO Apps and visit any SBI ATM / ADWM to withdraw the cash using the generated reference number and 6-digit YONO PIN. Under YONO Cash, minimum withdrawal is Rs.500/- with a maximum limit of Rs. 20,000/- (in multiples of Rs.500/-) per account, per day. The maximum permissible limit and number of withdrawals using YONO Cash will be over and above the existing limits permitted for cash withdrawal using Debit Cards.
QR Based Card-less cash withdrawal :
Similar to YONO Cash, SBI customer can use the YONO-Lite App (only) for QR Code based cash withdrawals at select ATMs and ADWMs. Customer can opt for Rs. 4000 or Rs. 2000 only. Daily Limit is Rs. 4000 per day i.e., 1 transaction of Rs. 4000 or 2 transactions of Rs. 2000 each through QR based withdrawal
UPI QR Cash (Card-less cash withdrawal):
Also known as Interoperable Cardless Cash Withdrawal (ICCW) / “UPI-ATM” feature is available at select ATMs / ADWMs. This functionality enables cash withdrawal through ATMs / ADWMs using UPI Applications that have been enabled for ICCW. Customers of SBI and any other Bank (live on ICCW) can withdraw cash through SBI ATMs using ‘UPI QR Cash’ facility. Maximum withdrawal limit is upto Rs.10,000 per day through maximum 2 transactions per day, using ICCW option. Amount can be withdrawn in multiples of Rs 100. Minimum transaction amount is Rs. 100.

Transfer of Savings Account to another Branch:
Using this service, customers can transfer their Savings Account from one branch to another, fulfilling the below eligibility at the time of submitting transfer request through machines:
a. Single Customer Information File (CIF) has only 1 Savings Account mapped to it.
b. Savings Account has been opened more than equal to 180 days ago.
c. The last Savings account transfer date should be more than 30 days.
d. KYC score of the customers should be greater than or equal to 110.

ATM Security Tips :
Get rid of dangers during ATM transactions !
Golden Rules to keep your ATM transactions safe and secure
Please sign on the reverse of the card immediately on receipt.
Change your card’s PIN regularly.
Never keep the PIN with the debit card or write on it. Always memorize it.
Do not provide the card and PIN details to anyone, including Bank / IBA / RBI / Govt. agency / family / friends, etc., Bank or any other institution will never ask for this information.
Do not let strangers come into the ATM room or take their help to complete the transactions.
Hide the keypad while keying in your PIN at ATM / POS terminals.
Do not throw your transaction slip in the ATM room as it contains your account information.
Always wait till the ATM returns to the idle mode and the green light is blinking.
Insist on swiping your card in your presence in hotels / shops / malls, etc.
Never swipe / tap your card at POS machines kept at unknown temporary stalls.
Block and destroy your old card, when you get a new one. Register your mobile number with the Bank to get SMS alerts for all transactions
Hotlist your card immediately on losing it by calling :

94491 12211 (Mobile number, Toll Free), OR

Customers - Be Aware:
Do's and Dont's of ATM Transactions.
Do's
Conduct your ATM transactions in complete privacy, never let anyone see you entering your PIN (Personal Identification Number) (ATM Password)
After completion of transaction, ensure that welcome screen is displayed on ATM screen
Ensure your current mobile number is registered with the Bank so that you can get alerts for all your transactions
Beware of suspicious movements of people around the ATM or strangers trying to engage you in conversation.
Do check if the card given to you by the merchant after completion of the transaction is your card
Look for extra devices attached to the ATMs that looks suspicious
Inform the Bank if the ATM / Debit card is lost or stolen, immediately to block it, report if any unauthorised transaction.
Check the transaction alert SMSs and Bank statements regularly
If cash is not dispensed and ATM does not display “cash out” please report to the Customer care of the Bank mentioned below.
Immediately check your phone for SMS for debit amount.
Dont's
Do not write your PIN on the card. It is advisable to memorise your PIN number
Do not take help from strangers or handover your card to anyone for using it
Do not disclose your PIN / OTP / card details like card number, expiry date, CVV / Internet Banking Passwords / UPI PIN / YONO Apps PIN / YONO Apps Password to anyone, including bank employees and family members
Do not allow the card to go out of your sight when you are making a payment.
Do not forget to collect your card from the machine after your transaction.
Avoid speaking on the mobile phone while you are transacting.
Do not wear helmets / sunglasses or cover face while transacting at machines.
