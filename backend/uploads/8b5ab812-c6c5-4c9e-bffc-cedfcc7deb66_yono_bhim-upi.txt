UPI (Unified Payment Interface) is an instant payment system which allows you to instantly transfer money to any bank account. BHIM UPI section is available under YONO Pay.
You will have to register on BHIM UPI on YONO using your bank registered mobile number and link your account. Once registered, you can use BHIM UPI for -
1. Pay to a UPI Id (VPA)
2. Pay to a bank account
3. Pay to a contact
4. Request money
5. Approve or reject Collect Request
5. Scan and Pay via QR Code
You can also manage your BHIM UPI profile on YONO. You can –
1. Create or Delete UPI Id
2. Add or Delete bank accounts linked to UPI Id
3. Set or Change or Reset UPI PIN for the bank accounts
4. View UPI transaction history
