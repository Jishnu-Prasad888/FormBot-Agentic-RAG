What is Digital Lending?
Digital Lending is a remote and automated lending process, largely by use of seamless digital technologies for customer acquisition credit assessment, loan approval, disbursement, recovery, and associated customer service.
Is our Bank compliant with the Digital Lending guidelines?
State Bank of India is compliant with Digital Lending Guidelines issued by RBI vide circular No RBI/2025-26/36 DOR.STR.REC.19/21.07.001/2025-26 dated 08.05.2026
