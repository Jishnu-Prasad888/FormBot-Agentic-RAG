Using YONO you can manage various services pertaining to your account. You can –
1) Activate SMS alerts in your account
2) Change your account type from Non-Cheque Book to Cheque Book one
3) Manage nominee for your PPF account
4) Change Home branch of your account
5) Manage e-statements
6) Disable/Enable UPI
