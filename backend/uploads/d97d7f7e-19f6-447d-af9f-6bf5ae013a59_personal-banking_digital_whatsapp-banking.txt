Which languages are supported on SBI WhatsApp Banking?
SBI WhatsApp Banking services are currently available in
English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati and Punjabi
to ensure wider accessibility and customer convenience.
Are there any charges for using WhatsApp Banking services?
SBI does not levy any
service charges
for using SBI WhatsApp Banking.
What are the operating hours for SBI WhatsApp Banking?
SBI WhatsApp Banking services are available
24x7x365
, including weekends and public holidays enabling uninterrupted access to banking services.
Can NRI customers use SBI WhatsApp Banking?
Yes, SBI WhatsApp Banking services are available to
NRI customers
. To avail the service, NRI customers must register using their bank-registered mobile number and complete the prescribed registration process.
Can non-SBI customers access WhatsApp Banking?
Currently, SBI WhatsApp Banking services are available only to existing SBI customers. However, non-SBI customers may use the platform to explore banking products, services, and offers, as well as to locate SBI ATMs and Branch offices.
How can I verify that I am chatting with SBI’s official WhatsApp number?
The official SBI WhatsApp Banking number is
+91 90226 90226
. To confirm authenticity:
Look for the blue tick badge next to the number, which indicates a Verified Business Account.
SBI’s official WhatsApp profile will always display this verified business account badge.
How can I discontinue SBI WhatsApp Banking services?
To discontinue the service, simply type
“Deregister”
on the SBI WhatsApp Banking number. Further instructions will be provided to complete the de-registration process.
How can I re-register after de-registering from WhatsApp Banking?
To re-register, send “Hi” or “Hi SBI” to the official WhatsApp Banking number +91 90226 90226 and follow the guided registration process.
Are specific keywords required for transactions on WhatsApp Banking?
No, predefined keywords are not required. Customers can simply type, tap, or send a voice note to access and avail banking services across various products.
Will SBI WhatsApp Banking request for sensitive information such as Customer ID, CIF number, Account number, or CVV?
No, SBI WhatsApp Banking will never ask for complete CIF number, full account number, or CVV. For selected services, only limited details such as the last four digits of the account number may be requested for processing requests.
