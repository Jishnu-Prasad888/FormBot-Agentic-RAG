NRI Portfolio Investment Services
You don't need to be in India to trade on the Indian stock exchange.
SBI's NRI Portfolio Investment Scheme (NRI PIS) Account enables you to buy and sell shares, debentures of Indian companies on the Indian Stock Market. Portfolio Investment Services are offered in selected branches of State Bank of India. The branch will grant you the permission to invest in stock market and all sale / purchase transactions will be routed through this branch. SBI also offers the added convenience of holding and trading in shares online, through its Demat Services.
