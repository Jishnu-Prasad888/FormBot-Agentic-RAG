i. Purpose
To provide liquidity support to existing Borrowers who are facing liquidity stress/ mismatches due to on-going West Asia Conflict.
ii. Eligibility
All borrowers (MSMEs & non-MSMEs) having:
Fund Based Working Capital limit in “Standard Category” (excluding SMA-2) as on 31.03.2026.
Borrower not to be NPA as on date of Sanction/Disbursement
iii. Quantum of Finance
Up to 20% of the peak fund-based working capital outstanding during the entire fourth quarter of FY 2025-26 with a maximum of Rs. 100 Cr. per borrower (except Airline sector)
Up to 100% of the total peak credit outstanding (fund-based and non-fund based) during the entire fourth quarter of FY 2025-26 with a maximum of Rs.1500 Cr. for Airline Sector.
iv. Nature of Facility
:
Working Capital Term Loan.
v. Repayment
MSMEs/Non MSMEs (except Airline sector)
: 5 years from the date of first disbursement including moratorium of 1 year
Airline Sector:
7 years from the date of first disbursement including moratorium of 2 years
vi. Margin
NIL (except Airline sector)
vii. Rate of Interest
MSMEs
– EBLR+0.75%, Max 9% p.a.
Non-MSMEs
– 3 M MCLR+0.75%, Max. 9%p.a.
viii. Charges
Processing Charges
: Nil.
Pre-payment penalty
: Nil.
Other Charges
: Nil.
ix. Security
Charge on existing securities (primary as well as collateral) and on assets created out of the loan under ECLGS 5.0.
x. Guarantee
Covered by NCGTC with NIL Guarantee fee. (Coverage: 100% for MSMEs & 90% for Non-MSMEs)
