Steps to be followed in YONO app
YONO Pre-login:
YONO app Pre-login-> Tap on YONO Cash
YONO Post login: Login to YONO app -> MPIN/User Id & Password-> Landing Page-> Tap on YONO Cash
Under the “Create Request” tab, select "Deposit Cash", choose yourself or existing SBI beneficiary account you want to deposit cash.
Review the transaction details, accept the terms and conditions, and confirm.
Once the request is processed, you’ll receive a transaction reference number via SMS to your registered mobile number.
Steps to be followed at the CDM/ADWM
Visit the SBI CDM, click on YONO CASH->YONO Cash Deposit and enter the transaction reference number received through SMS.
Review the deposit account details and insert the cash in CDM machine
After clicking "Enter" button the transaction is processed and the cash gets credited to the account. The amount of cash that can be deposited is as per the limit set at CDM end. Currently it is set at Rs. 49,900 per transaction
