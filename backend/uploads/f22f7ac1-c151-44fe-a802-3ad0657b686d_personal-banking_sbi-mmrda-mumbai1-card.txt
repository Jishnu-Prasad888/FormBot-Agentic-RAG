About SBI MMRDA Mumbai1 Card
State Bank of India and Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA) introduces to you Mumbai1 Card.
State Bank MMRDA Mumbai1 Card, is a contactless dual interface (EMV chip based) prepaid card with stored value functionality for providing a safe, secure, and seamless way of payment. Apart from a simple way to pay fares in the Metro, this card offers extended usage in buses as well. Compartmentalization of amount for different utilities such as transit amongst others is a unique feature of this State Bank Mumbai1 Card, which can be used for loading of passes for metro as well as buses, using the store value of the card.
State Bank MMRDA Mumbai1 Card offers many exciting features and a host of benefits for all your payment needs
Cashless travels in Metros
Purchase tickets and store passes for your Metro travel needs.
Purchase tickets for the buses using the same card
Chip-protection to ensure better security for your card
Easy to use Tap-and-Pay functionality

FAQ
Q. What is SBI - MMRDA MUMBAI1 card?
Ans.
This is a State Bank of India and Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA) co-branded prepaid card, having a contactless dual interface (EMV chip), with stored value functionality for making Tap-and-Go payments at MMRDA Metro Stations.
Q. What is MTS card?
Ans. Mass Transit Systems (MTS) card is a specific category of Prepaid card enabled only for payments across various modes of public transport such as metro, buses, rail, & waterways, tolls and parking. These Cards can be issued without KYC verification of the holders. MTS cards will be restricted to Transit use-cases only, operating in offline transaction mode and the amount outstanding, in such Cards shall not exceed Rs. 3,000/- at any point of time.
Note: Threshold limit for Chip/Global Balance on the card is a maximum of Rs. 2000/- only.
Q. How can I get MUMBAI1 Card?
Ans
i) To get Minimum Details Mumbai1 Card you just have to follow the below mentioned simple steps:
Visit your nearest MMRDA Metro Lines 2A or 7 station and request for the Mumbai1 Minimum Details Card at the Ticket Counter.
Fill your details in a simple Application Form and submit it to the operator.
After entering the details by the operator, One Time Pin (OTP) will be generated and sent to your mobile number provided in the application.
After OTP validation, pay the top-up amount to load balance on the card for usage.
Even you can fill required details in the webpage https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in/web/#/apply-card & note down reference number.
Give the reference number to ticket operator to get your Mumbai1 Card.
(ii) To get Mass Transit System (MTS) Mumbai1 Card you just have to follow the below mentioned simple steps:
Visit your nearest MMRDA Metro Lines 2A or 7 station and request for the Mumbai1 MTS Card at the Ticket Counter.
Provide your Mobile number to the operator.
After entering the Mobile number by the operator, One Time Pin (OTP) will be generated and sent to your Mobile number.
After OTP validation, pay the top-up amount to load balance on the card for usage.

Q.How can I use my MUMBAI1 Card at MMRDA Metro Lines 2A & 7 stations?
Ans.
MUMBAI1 Card has a unique feature of Stored Value balance which can be used across MMRDA Metro Lines 2A & 7 stations. Transactions in off-line mode are executed from the Stored Value balance in the card. Step-by-step procedure to be followed for using MUMBAI1 Card for Metro rides is as under:
Visit your nearest MMRDA Metro Lines 2A & 7 Station. Tap the card at the Entry Gate and as the gate opens, proceed to the platform for boarding the metro.
After completing the journey, tap the card again at the Exit Gate of destination station.
The fare amount for the journey undertaken will be debited from the stored value balance on the card.
Also, passes can be loaded on the specified service area onto the card, as per the business rule of the Metro authorities and can be used for metro rides as above. While travelling by using a pass, the fare amount for the journey will not be deducted as long as the pass is valid, as the value of pass shall be recovered upfront.
Q.How do I check the balance available on my Metro Card?
Ans.
Visit your nearest MMRDA Metro Lines 2A & 7 Station and tap your MUMBAI1 card on the Ticket Readers (TRs) installed at metro stations. Information regarding the card balance will be displayed on the screen.

Ans.
The threshold limit for Global Balance on the card is a maximum of ₹2000 (Rupees Two Thousand Only).
Q. What should I do if my card is Stolen, Lost or Damaged?
Ans.
If your card is lost, stolen or damaged, please follow the below mentioned steps:
Customers can block the card on Customer portal i.e
https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in
wherein customer can login using his/her credentials.
Report the information immediately at TOM Counter/Customer Portal or SBI Contact Centre 1800-8899 (toll free) or landline number 080-26599990 (toll) 24x7 and report the loss of card. The contact center executive will ask for identification details, e.g. Card Number, Mobile Number, date of birth, etc. On receipt of the information, your card will be blocked to ensure that it is not being used fraudulently by any unauthorized person. A ticket number will be sent to your registered mobile number after blocking the card.
Visit your nearest MMRDA Metro Lines 2A & 7 metro station for Card Replacement and submit a simple request letter for the same. After ensuring that your previous card has been blocked, a fresh replacement card will be issued to you.
In case of card not present scenario during replacement, global balance (offline/stores value balance) will not be transferred to the new card.
