How to Avail
Step 1: Log in to YONO and tap on the PAPL banner
Step 2: Enter your PAN, Date of Birth & Name on PAN
Step 3: Choose your desired loan amount, tenure, and EMI payment date.
Step 4: Accept the Terms & Conditions and confirm via OTP.
Your loan amount will be instantly credited to your Savings Bank account
