Purpose
To meet the cost of purchase of Combine Harvesters along with accessories.
Features
Loan Amount:
Minimum: Rs.5 lakhs and Maximum: Rs.35 lakhs
Margin:
20% of cost of Combine Harvester and accessories (excluding insurance and registration charges).
Repayment:
The loan is repayable as Principle Equated Distribution with Interest in half yearly Instalments.
The loan amount shall be paid directly to the authorized dealer / supplier.
Margin amount to be paid before or with the disbursement.
Eligibility Criteria
Individual, group of individuals such as JLGs / SHGs, etc.
Applicant should hold minimum of 3 acres of irrigated land for individual borrower and 5 acres of irrigated land for joint borrowers.
Or dry land of min. 15 acres.
Minimum CRIF Highmark Score above 600 or CIBIL score above 650 or No credit history.
Documents Required
Application form with photo of the borrowers.
Combine Harvester Quotation/ Proforma Invoice.
Proof of agricultural land/ cultivation.
Any other document as per sanction.
Interest
: One-year MCLR + 3.50%
Fees & Charges
: 1.50% of Loan amount
For more details, please visit nearest SBI Branch.
