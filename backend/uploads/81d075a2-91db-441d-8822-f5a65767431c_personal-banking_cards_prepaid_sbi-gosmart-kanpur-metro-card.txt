About SBI GOSMART KANPUR METRO CARD
State Bank of India and Uttar Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation (UPMRC) introduces to you GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card.
GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card is a contactless dual interface (EMV chip based) prepaid card with stored value functionality for providing a safe, secure, and seamless way of payment. A simple way to pay fares in the Metros, this card offers extended usage in Kanpur metro and in other transit projects across the country. Compartmentalization of amount for different utilities such as transit amongst others is a unique feature of this State Bank GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card, which can be used in NCMC enabled transit projects such as buses and parking, using the store value of the card.

FAQs
Q. What is SBI - GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card?
Ans
. This is a State Bank of India and Uttar Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation (UPMRC) co-branded prepaid card, having a contactless dual interface (EMV chip), with stored value functionality for making Tap-and-Go payments at UPMRC Metro Stations and NCMC enabled transit projects.
Q. How can I get GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card?
Ans. To get GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card you just have to follow the below mentioned simple steps:
Visit your nearest UPMRC Metro station and request for the GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card at the Ticket Counter.
Fill your details in the simple Application Form and submit it to the operator.
After entering the details by the operator, One Time Pin (OTP) will be generated and sent to your mobile number provided in the application.
After OTP validation, pay the top-up amount to load balance on the card for usage.
Even you can fill required details in the webpage
https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in/web/#/apply-card
& note down reference number.
Give the reference number to ticket operator to get your GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card.

Passport Number
Voter ID Number
Driving License Number
NREGA Job-card Number
Letter issued by the National Population Register, containing details of Name, Address, or Any other document as notified by the Central Government in consultation with the Regulator
Proof of possession of Aadhaar number
Q. How can I use my GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card at UPMRC Metro stations?
Ans
. GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card has a unique feature of Stored Value balance which can be used across UPMRC stations. Transactions in off-line mode are executed from the Stored Value balance in the card. Step-by-step procedure to be followed for using GOSMART Kanpur Metro Card for Metro rides is as under:
Visit your nearest UPMRC Metro Station. Tap the card at the Entry Gate and as the gate opens, proceed to the platform for boarding the metro train.
After completing the journey, tap the card again at the Exit Gate of destination station.
The fare amount for the journey undertaken will be debited from the stored value balance on the card.
Also, passes can be loaded on the specified service area onto the card, as per the business rule of the Metro authorities and can be used for metro rides as above. While travelling by using a pass, the fare amount for the journey will not be deducted as long as the pass is valid, as the value of pass shall be recovered upfront.
Q. How do I check the balance available on my Metro Card?
Ans. Visit your nearest UPMRC Metro Station and tap your GOSMART Kanpur Metro card on the Ticket Readers (TRs) installed at metro stations. Information regarding the card balance will be displayed on the screen.

Q. What should I do if my card is Stolen, Lost or Damaged?
Ans. If your card is lost, stolen or damaged, please follow the below mentioned steps:
Customers can block the card on Customer portal i.e.
https://prepaid.sbi.bank.in/
wherein customer can login using his/her credentials.
Report the information immediately at TOM Counter/Customer Portal or SBI Contact Centre 1800-1234 / 1800-2100 (toll free) and report the loss of card. The contact center executive will ask for identification details, e.g. Card Number, Mobile Number, date of birth, etc. On receipt of the information, your card will be blocked. A ticket number will be sent to your registered mobile number after blocking the card.
Visit your nearest UPMRC Metro station for Card Replacement and submit a simple request letter for the same. After ensuring that your previous card has been blocked, a fresh replacement card will be issued to you.
In case of card not present scenario during replacement, global balance (offline/stores value balance) will not be transferred to the new card.
