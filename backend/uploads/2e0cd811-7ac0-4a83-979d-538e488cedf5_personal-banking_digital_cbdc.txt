NON-FINANCIAL
a. GENERAL
1. What is a central bank digital currency (CBDC)
Central Bank digital currency (
CBDC
) or “
Digital Rupee
” or “
e₹
” is a digital form of currency notes issued by central bank. It is held in a CBDC wallet that is issued by the banks. The wallet can be linked to your SBI Bank account for loading
e₹
. It is not different from banknotes but being digital, it is likely to be easier, faster & cheaper. It has all the transactional benefits of other forms of digital money
2. What are the key features of e₹?
e₹
is a sovereign currency issued by Central Banks, in alignment with their monetary policy, as mentioned in the RBI concept note.
e₹
appears as a liability on the central bank’s balance sheet. It must be accepted as a medium of payment, legal tender, and a safe store of value by all citizens, enterprises, and government agencies.
e₹
is freely convertible against commercial bank money and cash.
3. Will e₹ replace cash, debit cards and credit cards?
No.
As per RBI,
e₹
is aimed to compliment and create an alternate to physical currency and not replace current forms of money. It will provide additional avenue of payment to users.
4. How e₹ is different from UPI or other fund transfer mode (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS)?
e₹
is a digital form of currency notes. However, other payments like UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS etc. uses underlying currency/cash to transfer the funds.
5. Is e₹ same as cryptocurrency such as bitcoin?
No. e₹
is digital form of currency notes unlike other cryptocurrencies such as bitcoin. e₹ has intrinsic value and is regulated by the central bank. e₹ will always have same value as of physical bank currency notes which is a legal tender issued by the central bank unlike crypto assets such as bitcoin.
6. Who can use e₹?
Anyone with an
e₹
App can transact in
e₹
through a convenient and safe digital wallet. This
e₹
wallet will be like your physical wallet in digital form on your Android/iOS device.
7. What is an e₹ Wallet?
An e₹ wallet is a digital wallet in your Android/iOS device. It stores digital currency just as a physical wallet stores physical currency.
8. What is CBDC - Interoperability?
The UPI interoperability feature by SBI Bank CBDC Wallet Application enables customers to make payments to beneficiary on their existing UPI QR Codes available with them, helping customers to transact their tokens and beneficiary to accept payments via UPI without any separate onboarding process.
9. How can I register for CBDC eRupee App of SBI?
You can register by downloading ‘eRupee App by SBI’ from Play Store/App Store and registering on it. Screen wise assistance is provided to guide you for registration.
For Bank account-linked Full-KYC eRupee wallet
, please keep ready your active savings/current account number and Debit Card ready for authentication. Give consent for all the required access and usage of eRupee App to take benefit of all the App features.
For Aadhaar-linked Minimum KYC eRupee wallet to received Govt. benefits
, please keep ready your Aadhaar number ready for authentication.

1. Why is my SIM verification failing?
SIM Card verification is done with mobile number registered in your SBI bank a/c. Please check the SIM Cards on your phone. If you have two SIM cards, kindly disable the other SIM card momentarily and keep active only the one that is registered in your SBI bank a/c. After verification and successful registration on the App, you may re-enable the other SIM.

c. PIN RELATED
1. How to reset PIN?
On the
e₹
App – Home Page, click on ‘Profile’ icon placed in the bottom.
Select ‘PIN settings’ and click on ‘Forgot PIN?’.
Enter last six digits and expiry date of the linked SBI Debit Card.
2. What if wrong Wallet PIN is entered during a transaction?
The Transaction will not proceed till the correct PIN is entered. Further, In case the customer forget his PIN, he may initiate resetting his Wallet PIN through by navigating through Profile > Pin setting > Forget PIN.
Please Note that a Cooling period will apply immediately after the Wallet PIN is reset, which is 24 hours for Android Phones and 48 hours on iOS.
