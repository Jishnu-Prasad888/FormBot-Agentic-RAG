SBI Rewardz is a way of adding value to the banking experience of our customers. An enterprise-wide Loyalty Program, SBI Reward Points to SBI customer for their everyday transactions across multiple banking channels.
At SBI Rewardz, we believe that you must be rewarded whenever you use SBI products or services. SBI Rewardz aims to reward you in multiple ways to enable you to reach your rewards goal as soon as possible.
From Bill Payments, YONO Cash transactions, booking tickets on IRCTC through YONO to opening and linking a SBI Capsec demat account, you earn Reward Points for a variety of transactions.
You can redeem your accumulated Reward Points to pay for a wide range redemption option
You can contribute your Reward Points into SBI Green Fund – started for sustainability causes. You can contribute anyone of the following cause:
• COVID-19
• Construction of Bio toilets
• Water Conservation
• Waste Management
• Renewable energy utility
• Tree plantation
