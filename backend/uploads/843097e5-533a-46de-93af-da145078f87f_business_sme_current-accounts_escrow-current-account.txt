Features
Free Monthly Cash Deposit limit: Rs.5,00,000/- per month including Home, Non-Home Branches, CDM/AWDM etc. Beyond free limits cards rates will be applied
Cash Deposit Limit at Non- Home Branches Maximum Rs. 5 lacs per day
Cheque Book facility: Not allowed.
Mode of Deposit: Deposit from all modes/channels are allowed.
ATM-Debit Card: Not allowed.
CINB: View rights only.
SMS Alerts charges: Nil
Standing instructions/ECS mandate: Not allowed
All other Charges applicable i.e., RTGS/NEFT, DD/BC etc.: As per card rates
Statement of account: Monthly free to Escrow related parties as per escrow agreement.
