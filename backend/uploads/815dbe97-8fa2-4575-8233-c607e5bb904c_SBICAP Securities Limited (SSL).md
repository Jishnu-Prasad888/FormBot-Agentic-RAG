When utilizing SBICAP Securities Limited (SSL), the loan ecosystem operates under a two-pronged structure.
First, SSL provides native, leverage-based trading loans directly through your Demat and trading account. Second, because it is a wholly-owned subsidiary, SSL acts as a direct sourcing and referral hub for the State Bank of India (SBI), allowing you to seamlessly secure retail loans backed by the parent bank. [1, 2, 3] 
------------------------------
## 1. Direct Leverage Loans via SSL (Trading & Demat Platform) [2] 
If you hold a Demat account with SBICAP Securities, you can access credit facilities to expand your purchasing power or unlock liquidity from your existing stock portfolio. [1, 4] 
## E-Margin / Margin Trading Facility (MTF)

* What it is: A leverage facility allowing you to buy up to 4 times the number of stocks you can afford by paying only a fraction of the cost upfront. SSL funds the remaining balance.
* Tenure: Positions can be held open for up to 365 trading days.
* Interest Holiday: On selected promotions, no interest is charged for the first 30 trading days; from Day 31 onward, interest applies (up to a regulatory cap of 24% p.a.).
* Risk Warning: If the stock value drops below your maintenance margin, SSL will trigger a margin call. Failure to deposit funds or add stock collaterals will result in the automated selling of your shares to recover the loan. [1, 5] 

## Loan Against Shares (LAS)

* What it is: An overdraft or demand loan facility backed directly by the equity shares sitting in your SBICAP Securities Demat account. This allows you to raise urgent personal liquidity without selling your investments.
* Loan Limits: Typically maps from ₹50,000 up to ₹20 Lakh per individual.
* Margin (LTV): Operates at a 50% margin requirement. For example, if you pledge approved shares worth ₹10 Lakh, you can access an overdraft line of up to ₹5 Lakh.
* Repayment Structure: Demand loans carry a maximum liquidation tenure of 30 months, while the Overdraft structure utilizes a running credit line with annual renewals. [4, 6, 7]

## How to Initiate a Loan

   1. For Trading Leverage (MTF): Simply toggle the "E-Margin" option on your order placement window inside the SBI Securities Portal when executing an intraday or delivery trade.
   2. For Pledging Shares: Log into your SSL platform, navigate to the "E-Pledge" dashboard, select your approved shares, and authorize the digital lien using the NSDL/CDSL OTP verification.
   3. For Bank Retail Loans: Head to the "Products" menu on the SSL platform and choose the respective loan category to auto-populate your verified KYC data over to the SBI banking portal. [1, 8, 9, 12, 13]

To give you the complete picture of how both systems work side-by-side, here is the granular breakdown. You can access Trading & Capital Market Loans directly inside your brokerage app to invest, and Retail Banking Loans using the brokerage platform as an integrated express checkout to trigger bank funding.
------------------------------
## 1. Trading & Capital Market Loans (Inside the SSL Platform)
These loans are issued or orchestrated directly by SBICAP Securities Limited (SSL) to fund your market activities or unlock immediate personal liquidity against your portfolio. [1] 
## A. E-Margin / Margin Trading Facility (MTF)

* Purpose: Boosts your stock purchasing capacity up to 4x. You pay a small margin upfront; SSL funds the rest.
* The "Zero Interest" Buffer: Enjoy 0% interest for the first 23 trading days on positions funded by SSL.
* Interest Rate: If you hold positions past Day 23, a standard rate of 0.04% per day (~14.6% p.a.) applies.
* Holding Window: You can hold your leveraged stock positions for up to 365 trading days. [2, 3] 

## B. Loan Against Shares (LAS) / Mutual Funds

* Purpose: Get a personal overdraft line of credit without liquidating your long-term wealth assets.
* Loan Value (LTV Ratio): Up to 50% of your verified equity stock valuation, and up to 70% for eligible Mutual Funds.
* Interest Rates:
* Against Shares: Typically ranges between 9.75% to 10.50% p.a.
   * Against Mutual Funds: Pegged natively at 9.95% p.a. (1.25% spread over 1-year MCLR).
* Minimum/Maximum Limits: Typically ranges from ₹50,000 up to ₹20 Lakh per individual asset bundle. [4, 5, 6, 7, 8] 

------------------------------

## A. SBI Home Loans

* Integration: Your active SSL portfolio speeds up your financial capability evaluation during underwriting.
* Interest Rates: Tied directly to SBI's current External Benchmark Based Lending Rate (EBR), scaling from 8.50% to 9.60% p.a. based on credit score.
* Perks: Features daily reducing balances and special lower interest concessions for women co-borrowers.

## B. SBI Car Loans

* Integration: SSL features fully digitized lead capture loops that push auto financing apps straight to local processing cells.
* Interest Rates: Typically averages between 8.75% to 9.80% p.a.
* Perks: Provides up to 7 years repayment terms with zero prepayment penalties on standard accounts. [1, 9] 

## C. SBI Education Loans

* Integration: Tailored for parents with high-value security asset holdings inside their SSL demat accounts.
* Interest Rates: Ranges from 8.55% to 10.50% p.a., with concessions available for premier domestic institutions (IIT/IIM) and globally ranked universities.
