Home Loans on YONO is a one-stop solution catering to all the home related needs like exploring property options, applying for loan, facilitating home furnishing etc. Existing SBI customers can generate their lead and in-principal approval for home loans on YONO.
Features
Available to customers 24*7. Instant in-principle approval and considerable improvement in overall TAT.
Real time notifications and status updates at every stage.
Low processing charges. No hidden cost or administrative charge

Eligibility
Resident Status: For Resident Indian only
Min Age : 18 years & Max. Age : 70 years
Loan Tenure: up to 30 years
How to Avail
For existing SBI customers, Log in to YONO & under Hamburger menu (☰ ) on the top left end > Loan > Home Loan, tap on Apply Now.
For non SBI customer, in pre login screen > Loans> Home Loan, tap on Apply Now
Complete the application and accept terms and conditions. Lead will be generated.
