NON – CALLABLE TERM DEPOSIT - BULK (NCD-B)
Eligibility:
Resident Individuals and Non- Individual Customers (Minors are excluded, separate scheme is available for NRI Customers)
Special Category of Customers :
Senior Citizens / Staff/ Staff Senior Citizens are NOT eligible for additional Interest Rate applicable over Non-Callable Rate for Bulk Deposit.
Minimum:
Rs.3,00,00,000/- (Rupee three crore only) *In multiples of Rs.1000/- thereafter.
Maximum:
No Limit
Tenor(s):
7 days to less than 3 years
Rate of Interest:
Mark up over Bulk Deposit card rates as decided by Bank
Eligible Products
TDR/ STDR only
Not available under any other schemes of Term Deposit.
Interest Pay out Option
Monthly/ Quarterly / Half yearly/Yearly Interest Pay-out is available for TDR.
For STDR, Interest shall be paid on maturity along with principal.
Premature Withdrawal:
Strictly not allowed
except in the following cases:
If directed by Court/ Regulator or in case of Winding up/ Bankruptcy.
Death of Individual Depositor(s)
Right to set off by SBI
Loan Facility:
Demand Loan facility available. No Overdraft facility allowed.
TDS Application:
As per IT act
Available through Channel:
The product shall be available through Branch CBS channel only.
