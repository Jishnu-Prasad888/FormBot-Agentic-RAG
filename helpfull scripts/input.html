How is 80E calculated?

The principal and interest are the two components while repaying any loan, including an education loan. To calculate 80E, you will first need to know the principal amount and the applicable interest.

Based on this, you will get the EMI you will be repaid monthly.

For example: Let us assume your EMI is for Rs. 15,000 per month, out of which your interest component amount is Rs. 7500. This would mean that you are paying Rs. 90,000 as interest for a fiscal year. This amount would be reduced from your taxable income for eight years or until the loan is repaid.
